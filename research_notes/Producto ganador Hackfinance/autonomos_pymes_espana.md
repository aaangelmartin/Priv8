# Financial and tax pain points of Spanish autónomos and pymes (Nov 2026), who solves them, and where a portal-operating AI agent could win

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. About 20 searches and fetches. Several primary sites (emprendedores.es, fieldfisher.com) returned 403, so some 2026 facts rest on secondary sources. This is flagged inline.

## 1. Regulatory deadlines: Verifactu, TicketBAI, SII, B2B e-invoicing (Crea y Crece)

### Takeaway
Verifactu has been postponed again and again: 2025 → 2026 → 2027 (RDL 15/2025), and Hacienda announced in Oct 2026 that it will move to Oct 2028, aligned with B2B e-invoicing. That announcement may not yet be in the BOE. Mandatory B2B e-invoicing is now fixed by RD 238/2026 and Orden HAC/1028/2026: 6 Oct 2027 for turnover above €8M, and 6 Oct 2028 for everyone else (all ~3.5M autónomos and the rest of the pymes). For 2026–2028 the urgency is mostly "prepare and choose software". It is not yet "fines now".

### Cited Findings
- Real Decreto-ley 15/2025 (BOE 3 Dec 2025) amended the fourth final provision of RD 1007/2023. Taxpayers under art. 3.1.a (Impuesto sobre Sociedades) must have adapted systems by 1 Jan 2027, and everyone else (autónomos/IRPF) by 1 Jul 2027 — [RSM alert](https://www.rsm.global/spain/sites/default/files/media/VeriFactu%20aplazado%20hasta%202027.pdf); [Legal Today](https://www.legaltoday.com/actualidad-juridica/noticias-de-derecho/nueva-prorroga-verifactu-no-sera-obligatorio-hasta-2027-para-sociedades-y-otros-contribuyentes-2025-12-04/)
- The earlier dates (1 Jan / 1 Jul 2026) had come from RD 254/2025 — [Legal Today](https://www.legaltoday.com/actualidad-juridica/noticias-de-derecho/nueva-prorroga-verifactu-no-sera-obligatorio-hasta-2027-para-sociedades-y-otros-contribuyentes-2025-12-04/)
- The obligations for software producers and sellers stayed in force despite the delay — [Noticias Jurídicas](https://noticias.juridicas.com/actualidad/noticias/20735-nueva-prorroga:-verifactu-no-sera-obligatorio-hasta-2027-para-sociedades-y-otros-contribuyentes/)
- **Oct 2026: a fourth postponement was announced, to October 2028**, aligning Verifactu with B2B e-invoicing. Reports call it "el cuarto aplazamiento en tres años" — [wwwhatsnew 6 Oct 2026](https://wwwhatsnew.com/2026/10/06/hacienda-verifactu-octubre-2028-aplazamiento-factura-electronica-2026); [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/es-es/locations/espana/actualidad/verifactu-aplazamiento-octubre-2028) (title only; 403); [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/gestion/aplazamiento-verifactu-2028/) (title only)
  - CONFLICT / UNCERTAINTY: Loentiendo says this is so far only an announcement that still needs a new royal decree amending RD 1007/2023 — [Loentiendo](https://loentiendo.com/aplazamiento-verifactu-2028/). Getrenn says the legal dates stay at 1 Jan 2027 / 1 Jul 2027 until the norm changes — [Getrenn](https://getrenn.com/es/blog/verifactu-aplazado). Diario de León's headline says "octubre de 2027", which may refer to the >€8M group — [Diario de León](https://www.diariodeleon.es/economia/261006/2099460/hacienda-retrasa-verifactu-octubre-2027-hacerlo-coincidir-factura-electronica_amp.html). Verify on boe.es.
- The underlying Ley Antifraude 11/2021 (ban on "doble uso" software) remains in force — [captain.legal](https://www.captain.legal/es/blog/verifactu-calendario-sociedades-autonomos/)
- B2B e-invoicing: RD 238/2026 (approved at the Council of Ministers 24–25 Mar 2026, BOE 31 Mar 2026) implements art. 2 bis of Ley 56/2007 as amended by Ley 18/2022 Crea y Crece — [EY](https://www.ey.com/es_es/technical/alertas-fiscal-legal/el-gobierno-aprueba-el-nuevo-sistema-espanol-de-facturacion-electronica-entre-empresarios); [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/latam/fiscalidad/art/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria-b2b)
- Orden HAC/1028/2026 (dated 2 Oct, BOE 5 Oct 2026, in force 6 Oct 2026) sets the dates. B2B e-invoicing is mandatory from **6 Oct 2027** for turnover above €8M, and from **6 Oct 2028** for all other businesses and professionals. Reporting of invoice payment status for those under €8M starts **6 Oct 2029**. The order also creates a "Solución pública de facturación electrónica" — [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/es/latam/fiscalidad/art/facturacion-electronica-obligatoria-b2b)
  - Older reading (EY April 2026 alert, before the Order): above €8M by 1 Jul 2027, the rest two years later — [EY PDF](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/es-es/technical/alertas-fiscal-legal/documents/ey-26-04-09-alerta-informativa-el-gobierno-aprueba-el-futuro-sistema-espanol-de-facturacion-electronica.pdf). The Oct Order takes precedence.
- TicketBAI / Batuz (Basque Country): Bizkaia phased Batuz in. It was mandatory only for large companies from 1 Jan 2024, with full roll-out (professionals, non-profits, certain sectors) by 1 Jan 2026. Source is secondary and undated — [fiscal-requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/1993). TicketBAI does not exempt from SII. It only covers the issued-invoices book — [fiscal-requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/2459)
- SII: taxpayers on SII-IVA (turnover above €6M, REDEME, groups) are excluded from the Verifactu-type regime — [Cuatrecasas table PDF](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/resources/facturacion-text-6ac3c925457dd327014276.pdf) (the table is ambiguous; verify)
- Number of people affected: RETA passed 3.5M for the first time in Oct 2026 (3,501,598, +2.3% year on year) — [Demócrata](https://www.democrata.es/economia/ata-vincula-el-aumento-de-autonomos-a-los-extranjeros-que-suponen-el-80-de-las-nuevas-altas/) (secondary). Nov 2025 official figure: 3,427,515 — [MITES AUT 11/2025](https://www.mites.gob.es/estadisticas/AUT/AUT_11_2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Repeated postponements create "compliance fatigue" and confusion: the legal date and the announced date differ. A product that explains "what applies to me today" has value. But Verifactu/e-invoicing are being commoditised by invoicing software (Holded, Quipu, Billin, Contasimple and others all market Verifactu readiness). Building a pure invoicing play here is a red ocean.
- The new public e-invoicing solution, and invoice-status reporting from 2029, will create new AEAT-side web portals and flows. These are future targets for portal-operating agents.

### Gaps
- No BOE text found for the Verifactu 2028 postponement. Its legal status is uncertain as of 10 Nov 2026.
- No official 2026 status from the Basque/Navarra foral haciendas (BOB/BOG/BOTHA).
- No DIRCE count of pymes was retrieved.

## 2. The costliest recurring obligations (time, money, sanctions)

### Takeaway
ATA estimates 226 hours a year per autónomo of bureaucracy (about €3,390/year, about €11.8bn for the whole group). Gestorías charge about €30–150/month. The sharpest risk points are the RETA cuota regularisation by real income (millions affected, errors, notifications missed through gestorías) and electronic notifications (DEHú/NEO). With notifications, missing the 10-day window counts as being notified and starts the payment and appeal deadlines.

### Cited Findings
- ATA (Dec 2025): about 200 h/year per autónomo, €3,000/year at €15/h; more than 650M hours and about €10bn for the group — [The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/2025-12-18/ata-autonomos-horas-burocracia/); [El Español/esdiario](https://www.esdiario.com/economia/251218/175118/ata-denuncia-autonomos-dedican-200-horas-anuales-burocracia_amp.html)
- ATA (Aug 2026): 226 h/year, €3,390/year per autónomo; more than 780M hours and €11,770M/year in total. The figure includes coordination with the gestoría or software. There is no breakdown by task — [Diario de León](https://www.diariodeleon.es/economia/260811/2093911/burocracia-cuesta-autonomo-3-400-euros-ano.html); [Artículo14](https://www.articulo14.es/economia/el-coste-de-la-burocracia-para-los-autonomos-se-dispara-casi-400-euros-el-ultimo-ano-20260811.html); [finantres](https://finantresnoticias.com/negocios-y-autonomos/autonomos-dedican-226-horas-ano-tramites-donde-se-escapa-ese-tiempo/)
- Canarias autónomos report about 200 h/year of red tape — [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/autonomos-canarios-insoportable-burocracia-canarias-200-horas-ano-15-enero-2026/)
- ATA estimates autónomos would save €500M/year with a franchised VAT scheme. That gives a proxy for what VAT compliance (modelo 303/390) costs — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/economia/economia--ata-estima-que-los-autonomos-se-ahorrarian-500-millones-de-euros-anuales-con-el-sistema-de-iva-franquiciado--21529017.html)
- Gestoría prices (vendor sources, VAT excluded):
  - €65–150/month — [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/gestoria-precios); also [Billeo 2026](https://www.billeo.es/blog/cuanto-cobra-gestoria-autonomo-precio-2026)
  - €60–150/month — [Trustlocal](https://trustlocal.es/coste/asesores-fiscales-coste/gestoria-para-autonomos-precios/)
  - Tiered: basic €30–50, full €50–80, with employees €80–150 — [OIE](https://www.oie.es/gestorias-online-para-autonomos)
  - By volume: €40–80 for up to 50 invoices; €120–150 for 51–99 invoices — [Cronoshare](https://www.cronoshare.com/cuanto-cuesta/asesor-fiscal)
- **RETA cuota by real income: regularisation**
  - First regularisation (2023 income) covered more than 3.7M autónomos. 26.85% had paid above their bracket and 23.85% below; about half needed no adjustment. More than 429,377 people renounced refunds. Refunds were paid Mar–Apr 2025 — [Seguridad Social / Inclusión](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/w/la-seguridad-social-finaliza-con-exito-el-primer-proceso-de-regularizacion-de-cuotas-de-los-trabajadores-autonomos); [revista.seg-social](https://revista.seg-social.es/-/la-seguridad-social-finaliza-con-%C3%A9xito-el-primer-proceso-de-regularizaci%C3%B3n-de-cuotas-de-los-trabajadores-aut%C3%B3nomos)
  - About 800,000 underpaid and 460,000 overpaid in 2023 — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias_amp/economia/economialaboral--casi-800000-autonomos-cotizaron-menos-de-lo-debido-en-2023-y-460000-cotizaron-de-mas--19815806.html) (these figures differ from the official percentages; possibly different cut-offs)
  - The 2024-income regularisation resolutions went out in batches Jan–Feb 2026, with refunds by 15 Apr 2026 — [Declarando](https://declarando.es/blog/regularizacion-cuotas-autonomos); [InfoAutónomos](https://www.infoautonomos.com/blog/regularizar-cuotas-autonomo-seguridad-social/) (secondary)
  - ATA refused to meet Seguridad Social until the 2023 regularisation errors are fixed — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/economia/economia--ata-no-se-reunira-con-seguridad-social-hasta-que-no-solucione-errores-en-la-regularizacion-de-cuotas-de-2023--20234512.html)
  - Reported court ruling (TSJ Castilla y León, June 2026): a regularisation notified only to the gestoría was annulled, and the autónoma got a new deadline to choose her base. In 2026 some autónomos also lost their window to choose a base because only the gestoría saw the notice — [mutuafacil](https://mutuafacil.es/autonomos/regularizacion-cuotas-autonomos/) (single secondary source; unverified)
  - The tax effect depends on when the regularisation is notified (deductibility in IRPF) — [AECE revista](https://revista.aece.es/contable/89/pdf_369082d18ea20f82f5cf449b752fe61f6942c0fdd881417659824611.pdf); [Taxfix](https://taxfix.com/es-es/blog/declarar-las-cuotas-regularizadas-en-la-renta/)
- **Electronic notifications (DEHú / AEAT NEO / Seguridad Social)**
  - The 10 calendar days start when the notification is made available. If it is not accessed, it is deemed rejected and served, and payment and appeal deadlines start running (art. 43 Ley 39/2015) — [AEAT folleto NEO](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/folleto-actividades-economicas/11-notificaciones-electronicas/11_2-notificaciones-electronicas-obligatorias-neo.html); [Mallorca Diario](https://www.mallorcadiario.com/hacienda-puede-notificarte-en-festivo-claves-del-plazo-de-10-dias-en-baleares)
  - Up to 30 "días de cortesía" per year can be requested, with 7 days' notice — [Taxdown](https://taxdown.es/notificaciones-hacienda/noreply-dehu-correo-gob-es)
  - Who is obligated is confusing. Before the AEAT, autónomos are not obligated unless they belong to a professional college — [Declarando](https://declarando.es/blog/notificaciones-de-la-aeat-guia-para-autonomos). Before Seguridad Social almost all autónomos are — [Taxdown](https://taxdown.es/notificaciones-hacienda/noreply-dehu-correo-gob-es). Some sources say all autónomos are obligated — [Garona](https://www.garonabogados.es/?p=2251)
  - The DEHú access window is not the same as the deadline to appeal, pay or present evidence — [Legal Fournier](https://legalfournier.com/?p=4215)
- Renta campaign 2026 ran 8 Apr–30 Jun — [Declarando](https://declarando.es/blog/cuanto-se-paga-de-autonomo-el-primer-ano)

### Inferences
- The most "agentable" pain is not filing the 303/130 (software and gestorías do that). It is monitoring and triage across portals: DEHú, AEAT NEO, Seguridad Social (Import@ss/Sede), DGT DEV and ayuntamientos. A missed notification has hard legal consequences, and the gestoría-only-sees-it problem is documented.
- RETA regularisation is a recurring annual event, every year from 2025 on. It affects ~3.5M people, and around half get an adjustment each year. That makes it a high-frequency, money-relevant trigger: checking the resolution, claiming the refund, choosing the base and, where it applies, appealing.

### Gaps
- Not found: the % of autónomos using a gestoría (a figure of around 70–80% circulates, but no source was retrieved, so it is excluded).
- Not verified: recargos and sanctions for late filing (art. 27 LGT recargos and art. 191–198 sanctions) were not fetched this session. Check them on agenciatributaria.es.
- No study found of time per modelo (303, 130, 111, 115, 349, 347, 390).

## 3. Startups addressing this, and what remains manual

### Takeaway
The Spanish market splits into invoicing/accounting SaaS (Holded, Quipu, Billin, Contasimple, Anfix, Txerpa) and online gestorías (Declarando, Xolo, TaxDown, Taxfix), at €0–€50+/month. Nearly all file AEAT modelos through colaboración social or apoderamiento, using humans or AEAT web services. AI-native players (Kabilio, Bcount; YC's Tabula, Cifrato, Last Accounting Company) focus on bookkeeping, reconciliation and accountant workflows. No evidence was found of any startup that markets an agent operating the AEAT, Seguridad Social or DEHú web portals end-to-end on the user's behalf.

### Cited Findings
- Declarando: plans with tax filing from about €49.90/month + VAT (annual billing); the cheaper plans are software only — [Xolo vs Declarando (competitor source)](https://blog.xolo.io/es-us/xolo-vs-declarando)
- Xolo: full tax-management plans from €49/month + VAT; covers Canarias — [Xolo vs TaxDown](https://blog.xolo.io/es/xolo-vs-taxdown-que-gestoria-online-es-mejor-para-autonomos-en-2026)
- TaxDown: price depends on profile (autónomo, investments, international); excludes Canarias according to Xolo — [TaxDown pricing](https://taxdown.es/precio/); [Xolo](https://blog.xolo.io/es/xolo-vs-taxdown-que-gestoria-online-es-mejor-para-autonomos-en-2026)
- Comparison of TaxDown, Taxfix and a traditional gestoría on price — [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/taxdown-vs-taxfix-vs-gestoria-cual-sale-mas-barato-2026)
- Holded, Quipu and Billin all publish Verifactu / e-invoicing compliance content, which shows they are positioned on it — [Holded](https://www.holded.com/es/blog/ley-crea-y-crece); [Quipu](https://getquipu.com/blog/factura-electronica-b2b/); [Billin](https://www.billin.net/blog/?p=29361)

  - The article does not mention AEAT filings or portal automation.
  - Competitor: Ravical (Belgium, $17M seed).
  - [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/kabilio-raises-e10m-to-bring-ai-agents-to-accounting-firms); earlier €4M round — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/kabilio-e4-million-raised-to-expand-ai-platform-for-accounting-firms-in-spain)

- YC:
  - **Tabula** (S24, Berlin): AI finance platform for SMEs and their accountants in Europe — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tabula)
  - **Cifrato** (W25, Bogotá): "AI Accountant for Accounting Firms", covering invoice capture, reconciliation and "tax filings"; it names no specific portal — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/cifrato)
  - **Last Accounting Company**: "agent-native accounting firm" — [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/last-accounting-company)
  - **Invofox** (S22, Madrid): document-processing API — [YC finance list](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/finance-and-accounting)
  - **Caribou**: "autonomous tax firm"; $2.7M seed including YC — [Runtimewire](https://runtimewire.com/article/juan-andrade-autonomous-tax-firm-seed-2-7m)

- Gestoría-side AI tooling: CopilotGestoría blogs on AEAT apoderamiento expiry (1 April 2026 extension and caducidad) and gestoría pricing studies. This points to a market for tools aimed at gestorías — [CopilotGestoría apoderamientos](https://copilotgestoria.com/blog/apoderamientos-aeat-2026-prorroga-caducidad-1-abril-guia-gestorias); [pricing](https://copilotgestoria.com/blog/pricing-gestorias-2026-tarifas-servicio-margenes-rentabilidad-estudio-despachos)

### Inferences
- What remains manual even with these tools:
  - pulling notifications from DEHú, NEO and Seguridad Social and deciding what to do with them;
  - reviewing the RETA regularisation and claiming;
  - applying for aplazamientos/fraccionamientos and following them up;
  - downloading certificates (al corriente AEAT/TGSS, needed for subsidies and public tenders);
  - ayuntamiento taxes (IAE, IBI, plusvalía, tasas) on heterogeneous municipal sedes;
  - DGT fines;
  - bank portals (certificates, disputes, reconciliation exports where PSD2 does not cover them).
- Incumbents rely on colaboración social (bulk filing) and apoderamientos. A newcomer agent could instead sell to gestorías ("Kabilio for portals") or straight to autónomos with a delegated-access model.

### Gaps
- Abaq: nothing found; possibly a misnamed company. Txerpa, Anfix and Contasimple pricing and features were not fetched.
- No public evidence found either way on whether any Spanish startup uses RPA or browser agents on the AEAT or Seguridad Social sedes. Some gestoría tools are suspected to, but this is unverified.

## 4. Ayudas y subvenciones (BDNS, Kit Digital, NextGen) and reclamaciones (banks, Hacienda)

### Takeaway
NextGen/PRTR money must be paid by 31 Dec 2026. Kit Digital is winding down ("until funds run out", no new call as of Sep 2026), payments are backlogged and the government has a "plan de choque". Grant discovery is served (FANDIT, Ayudascan, Legálitas), but filing and justification on heterogeneous sedes remains manual. For bank complaints the single Autoridad de Defensa del Cliente Financiero is still not created (the bill was revived in Sep 2026), so complaints still go bank SAC → Banco de España.

### Cited Findings
- Kit Digital and Kit Consulting:
  - Kit Consulting (Red.es C012/24-SI) has a budget of €300M — [Santander guide](https://wcm.bancosantander.es/wce/documentos-cms/guia-presentacion-solicitudes_C01224_20240612.pdf)
  - The 2026 focus is execution and closure. Under Reg. (UE) 2021/241, 31 Dec 2026 is the payment deadline — [TeamSystem](https://teamsystem.es/magazine/fondos-europeos-2026/)
- Kit Digital has no fixed end date and runs until funds are exhausted (attributed to Orden TDF/39/2026, unverified). No new call was open as of Sep 2026 — [OIE](https://www.oie.es/kit-digital-autonomos/); [TeamSystem Kit Digital 2026](https://teamsystem.es/magazine/kit-digital-2026/)
- BOE 28 Jan 2026: aid already granted stays under its original bases until the files are closed — [BOE-A-2026-2069](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/01/28/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-2069.pdf)
- BOE 14 Jul 2026 measures:
  - a "plan de choque" to cut the backlog of pending payments, prioritising aid already justified;
  - tighter controls on digitalising agents and their related parties being beneficiaries.
  - [BOE-A-2026-15351](https://www.boe.es/boe/dias/2026/07/14/pdfs/BOE-A-2026-15351.pdf)
- Official Kit Digital evaluation report — [Red.es](https://redes-ondemand2.flumotion.com/redes/ondemand2/KitD/Informe_evaluacion-Kit-Digital-C2.pdf)
- FANDIT (Madrid, 2019): grant search, alerts and justification reports; mostly SME users; powers Legálitas' and Repsol Solred's grant finders — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/fandit); [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/gestion/fandit); [Legálitas](https://www.legalitas.com/negocios/subvenciones); [Repsol](https://www.repsol.es/autonomos-y-empresas/asesoramiento/buscador-de-subvenciones-para-empresas/)
- Ayudascan: uses BDNS data, updated every 6 hours — [Ayudascan](https://ayudascan.com/en)
- Claims that half of public subsidies go unrequested, and that companies took more than €11.5bn across 1,801 calls in summer 2024 (unverified) — [Merca2](https://www.merca2.es/2025/07/23/ia-subvenciones-publicas-2215924/); [Merca2](https://www.merca2.es/2025/10/06/ia-no-pierdan-subvenciones-2237343/)
- Founder quote: "no puede ser que siempre se las lleven los mismos" (grants concentrated among the same firms) — [El Referente](https://elreferente.es/?p=70774)
- Autoridad de Defensa del Cliente Financiero:
  - Not yet created. In Jul 2026 Minister Cuerpo said the bill would be revived in Sep 2026, aiming for approval by year end.
  - It would merge the Banco de España, CNMV and DGSFP complaint services and resolve bank complaints for free in 90 days.
  - [La Moncloa](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/serviciosdeprensa/notasprensa/economia-comercio-empresa/paginas/2026/240726-autoridad-deensa-cliente-financiero.aspx); [Moncloa.com](https://www.moncloa.com/2026/07/25/autoridad-defensa-cliente-financiero-septiembre-3405435/)
- Separate law, Ley 10/2025 on customer service departments: the maximum resolution time becomes one month — [Finreg360](https://finreg360.com/alerta/la-ley-que-regula-los-servicios-de-atencion-a-la-clientela-se-ha-promulgado/); [GA_P](https://ga-p.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Atencion_cliente_financiero.pdf)
- The consumer council (CCU) criticises the delay — [CCU 2026](https://consumo-ccu.consumo.gob.es/sites/default/files/notas-de-prensa/DIAMUNDIALDERECHOSPERSONASCONSUMIDORAS2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Before 31 Dec 2026, Kit Digital and Kit Consulting beneficiaries and agents face justification and payment-chasing deadlines on Red.es/Acelera pyme portals. This is a short, urgent window, but it disappears after 2026.
- Grant discovery is solved. Filing and justification are not: each convocatoria has its own sede, forms, certificates (al corriente AEAT/TGSS) and electronic signature. A portal agent is differentiated here.
- Bank complaints: the new one-month SAC deadline (Ley 10/2025), followed by escalation to Banco de España, is a structured, deadline-driven, web-form flow. It suits an agent that drafts, files and tracks.

### Gaps

## 5. Where a portal-operating agent (tokenised data, provenance, human hold, signed audit) adds unique value, and the legal question

### Takeaway
Spanish law already has two channels for acting on another's behalf at the AEAT: colaboración social (for professionals and firms in associations with an AEAT agreement) and apoderamiento (registered powers, granted via certificate, DNIe or Cl@ve). Neither explicitly covers an AI agent driving the user's own certificate or Cl@ve. The safest design has the agent prepare and navigate, with a human (the user, or a gestoría that is a colaborador social or apoderado) signing and submitting under a recorded hold, plus a signed audit trail.

### Cited Findings
- Colaboración social lets collaborators file third-party returns online (including returns with payment by direct debit) and aplazamiento requests, using the collaborator's own certificate — [AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/colaboracion-social-ayuda-tecnica/presentacion-declaraciones-colaboradores-sociales_.html); [AEAT aplazamientos colaboradores](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/consultas-informaticas/colaboracion-social-ayuda-tecnica/consulta-solicitudes-aplazamiento-colaboradores.html)
- Advisers normally join through a professional association that has an AEAT agreement — [fiscal-impuestos](https://www.fiscal-impuestos.com/asesor-fiscal-representante-colaboracion-social-representacion-voluntaria-registro-apoderamientos.html); [Cegid](https://www.cegid.com/ib/es/blog/que-es-un-colaborador-social/)
- Apoderamiento is separate from colaboración social. It is needed for things like DEHú notifications, status queries and follow-up of aplazamientos. It can be granted online with a certificate, DNIe or Cl@ve, or on paper — [Taxdown](https://taxdown.es/informacion-impuestos/poderdante-apoderado-hacienda); [Billeo](https://www.billeo.es/blog/autorizacion-representacion-aeat). Sources disagree slightly on scope.
- Apoderamiento renewals and expiries (1 Apr 2026) are an operational burden for gestorías — [CopilotGestoría](https://copilotgestoria.com/blog/apoderamientos-aeat-2026-prorroga-caducidad-1-abril-guia-gestorias)
- Notifications seen only by the gestoría have led to lost rights; one court annulled such a notice — [mutuafacil](https://mutuafacil.es/autonomos/regularizacion-cuotas-autonomos/) (secondary)

### Inferences
- Unique value areas, ranked by pain × absence of an API × lack of incumbents:
  1. **A unified notifications inbox and triage** across DEHú, AEAT, TGSS, DGT and ayuntamientos. It would extract deadlines, draft the reply or payment, and require a human hold before anything is accepted or filed. The 10-day rule and gestoría-only visibility make this both urgent and a liability.
  2. **A RETA regularisation copilot**: read the resolution in Import@ss, compare it with real IRPF net income, claim the refund, choose the base, and draft the alegación.
  3. **Certificates and paperwork** for subsidies and tenders (al corriente AEAT/TGSS, censal), plus filing and justifying grants on heterogeneous sedes (BDNS, Red.es, CCAA). The Kit Digital closure runs to 31 Dec 2026.
  4. **Aplazamientos/fraccionamientos** and **recursos de reposición** against sanctions and recargos.
  5. **Bank complaints** under Ley 10/2025, with a one-month SAC clock and escalation to Banco de España.
- Safety architecture fits the law:
  - Tokenised data and provenance: prove which document or portal each figure came from.
  - Human hold before any signature with a certificado or Cl@ve, as acceptance and filing have legal effects (art. 43 Ley 39/2015).
  - Signed audit trail, giving evidence in disputes such as the "notified only to the gestoría" cases.
- Go-to-market: the legally cleanest route is to partner with, or be, a colaborador social or gestoría, or use registered apoderamientos. Having the agent hold the user's .p12 certificate or Cl@ve PIN carries security and legal-identity risk; Cl@ve terms tie the credential to its holder. Treat this as an inference that needs legal review.

### Gaps
- No AEAT or Seguridad Social rule found that explicitly permits or forbids automated or agentic use of a certificate or Cl@ve on the sede. Orden HAP/1545/2014 and the colaboración-social agreement texts were not reviewed.
- Not researched: the Seguridad Social Sistema RED autorizado regime and Import@ss representation rules, or ayuntamiento-level representation.
- No legal opinion found on AI agents and electronic signatures (eIDAS 2 / EUDI wallet) in Spain.
