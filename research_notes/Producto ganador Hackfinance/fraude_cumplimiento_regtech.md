# Fraud, compliance and regtech for financial institutions: where AI agents change the landscape (as of November 2026), and gaps an agent-control layer could fill

Scope note: EU and Spain focus, with UK, US and Hong Kong context. Most vendor facts come from vendor sites or trade press and are self-reported. Several regulatory dates below are expected dates, not confirmed Official Journal publications. Research was capped at about 17 tool calls, so some primary documents (the full EPC VoP rulebook v2.0, the final PSR text, Visa TAP and AP2 specifications) were not read directly.

## 1. Which YC (2023–2026) and EU startups do AML/KYC/fraud with AI agents, and what do they leave to humans?

### Takeaway
The AML/KYC "agentic analyst" segment is crowded and well funded: Bretton AI (ex-Greenlite), Arva, Diligent, Fenrock, Unit21, Sardine, Parcha and others. Almost all of them automate alert triage, KYB/KYC review and SAR drafting while keeping a human signing off on filings and final decisions. The newer, thinner segments are three: scam/social-engineering defence at the moment of payment (BeeSafe AI, Rulebase), Know Your Agent identity (Persona, Beltic, Baselayer, Skyfire, Trulioo), and controlling what a customer's agent is allowed to pay. Nobody in this sample combines mandate enforcement, provenance of the beneficiary data and signed evidence for disputes.

### Inferences
- The back-office AML/KYC agent market (alert triage, KYB, SAR drafting) is saturated, with at least five YC companies plus scaled incumbents. A hackathon product should not compete there head-on.
- What humans keep across these vendors: SAR/STR filing sign-off, final adjudication of onboarding, exception handling, and sampling-based quality assurance. The pattern is "the agent recommends, a human signs," which creates demand for tamper-evident evidence of what the agent saw and why it recommended something. Products such as Unit21 keep audit logs, but none is described as producing a cryptographically signed, portable receipt.
- KYA vendors answer "who is the agent and who stands behind it." They do not appear to answer "was this specific beneficiary or amount derived from trusted data or injected content." That provenance gap is open.

### Gaps
- I did not verify each listed company's YC batch on its YC page (Alloy, Persona, Socure, Feedzai, BioCatch, Hawk AI, Sumsub, Unico, Duna, Footprint, Cascade and Vesta were not researched individually within the call budget). Most of these are not YC companies; Persona, Alloy and Socure are scaled incumbents.
- I found no independent audits or regulator evaluations of any of these agent products.

## 2. EU Verification of Payee: how it works, its limits, the scams it does not stop, and PSR impersonation-fraud refunds in 2026

### Takeaway
VoP has been mandatory for euro-area PSPs since 9 October 2025. It is a free name/IBAN check before authorisation that returns match, close match, no match or "other." It is decision support only: a "no match" does not block the payment, and a payer who proceeds after a warning bears the liability. It does nothing when the victim is persuaded to pay a correctly named account (mules, "safe account" scams, investment and romance fraud, AI-written invoices to real-looking entities). The PSR, politically agreed in November 2025 with Official Journal publication expected around end-2026, limits the new impersonation refund to impersonation of the payer's own PSP. Expected application is around H1 2028.

### Cited Findings
- VoP has been mandatory since 9 October 2025 for euro-area PSPs before authorising any euro credit transfer, standard or instant, and is free to the payer. Possible outcomes are match, close match, no match and other. A "no match" does not block the payment, which makes VoP "a decision-support mechanism rather than an automatic fraud filter." If the payer proceeds after a warning, liability shifts to the payer — [Fintech Weekly, 2026](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026); legal basis Regulation (EU) 2024/886 (Instant Payments Regulation) — [EUR-Lex 2024/886](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/886/oj)
- The EPC VoP scheme went live on 5 October 2025 — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/regulations/news/epcs-verification-of-payee-scheme-rulebook-enters-into-effect); [Trustpair](https://trustpair.com/gb/?p=15025)
- Rulebook v1.1 has applied since 20 September 2026. v2.0 is envisaged for the end of November 2026 and would add a definitive inquiry and compensation process — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- Trustpair, citing EPC data via Qombo (secondary), reports about 2.2 billion VoP checks in Q2 2026, with roughly one in three not returning a clean match — [Trustpair](https://trustpair.com/gb/?p=15025)
- Only non-consumers submitting payments in a batch can opt out. Deutsche Bank's EBICS client data for January 2026 showed 80% of SCT files and 67% of SCT Inst files opted out (one bank, not market-wide). A single no-match or close-match can hold an entire batch for manual intervention. The ECB's March 2026 strategy notes that VoP implementation in corporate ERPs lags — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- Timeline: euro-area PIs and EMIs must send and receive instant euro transfers from 9 April 2027, and non-euro-area PSPs (including VoP) from 9 July 2027. In a Celent survey, one in five EMIs/PIs expected to miss July 2027 by three to six months, and 61% of banks expected compliance spend above €20M — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- Fraud context: EU payment fraud rose from €3.5bn in 2023 to €4.2bn in 2024 (EBA/ECB). Instant-payment fraud volumes grew 175% over the three years covered, against 98% growth in transaction volumes. SCT Inst was about 35.6% of euro credit-transfer volume in Q1 2026 — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- Limits: VoP does not stop a victim socially engineered into ignoring a mismatch, mule accounts with legitimate-looking details, or romance and impersonation scams where the name matches — [AMLWatcher](https://amlwatcher.com/blog/verification-of-payee/). Virtual and collection accounts are a known gap, because the check may resolve to the master account, not the sub-account — [ABE-EBA](https://www.abe-eba.eu/?p=3386). AI-generated, convincing invoices and payment requests are a rising threat — [LuxHub on EPC fraud trends](https://luxhub.com/insights-from-the-latest-epc-fraud-trends-report-2-iban-manipulation-invoice-fraud-the-growing-need-for-payee-verification/)
- PSR status (as of August 2026): political agreement November 2025, Council and ECON endorsement done (COREPER on 22 April 2026, ECON May 2026), plenary and formal adoption pending at that date. Official Journal publication was expected in H2 2026. General application is estimated at D+21 months (about H1 2028) and the VoP extension to all credit transfers at D+27 months (about H2 2028) — [Sopra Steria](https://www.soprasteria.com/insights/details/payment-services-regulation-psr-in-2026--the-state-of-play-for-banks); [PwC Legal April 2026](https://legal.pwc.de/content/services/regcore-client-alert/pwc-client-alert-eus-new-payments-framework-april-2026.pdf); [Taylor Wessing Nov 2025](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2025/11/eu-lawmakers-strike-a-deal-on-payments-reforms). Fintech Weekly expects Official Journal publication in December 2026 or January 2027 — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- PSR impersonation refund: covers only impersonation of the customer's own PSP (staff or PSP-attributed channels: phone, email, app). Impersonation of authorities, investment scams and romance fraud are out. The customer must notify without undue delay and file a police report. The PSP has 15 business days to refund in full or refuse in writing with ADR details, and may refuse only on fraud or gross negligence. Burden of proof is on the PSP, and a successful SCA alone no longer proves negligence — [Sopra Steria](https://www.soprasteria.com/insights/details/payment-services-regulation-psr-in-2026--the-state-of-play-for-banks); [Finologee](https://finologee.com/psr-what-the-provisional-political-agreement-signals-for-psps/). Some earlier sources cite 10 business days, probably from an earlier draft — [Mondaq](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/white-collar-crime-anti-corruption-fraud/1745300/psd3-and-the-psr-rewriting-fraud-liability-in-eu-payments-law)
- Other PSR fraud provisions: transaction monitoring becomes a standalone obligation for both the payer's PSP (before execution) and the payee's PSP (before funds are made available). The payee's PSP may block on "objectively justified" suspicion and must block where evidence is "clear and incontrovertible." Platforms (DSA VLOPs) and telecom operators must act against spoofing and fraudulent financial ads, and banks that refund can seek recovery from them. If VoP is missing or defective, the payer's PSP is liable — [Sopra Steria](https://www.soprasteria.com/insights/details/payment-services-regulation-psr-in-2026--the-state-of-play-for-banks)
- UK contrast: the PSR (UK Payment Systems Regulator) mandatory APP reimbursement regime has applied since 7 October 2024. Not re-verified in this research; see Section 3 for the UK suggestion to extend APP reimbursement to agent-driven fraud — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)

### Inferences
- The highest-value scam gap after VoP is the "correct name, wrong intent" payment: an authorised payment to a matching, legitimate-looking account under manipulation. VoP returns "match," so the bank's best signal says "safe." This is where conversational/voice scam detection and provenance of how the payee data reached the payer (pasted from an SMS, read from an email, typed after a phone call) add signal.
- Because the PSR puts the burden of proof on the PSP and rules out SCA as sufficient proof, banks need contemporaneous evidence of what the customer (or their agent) saw, which VoP result was shown, and whether a warning was overridden. A signed receipt that binds VoP result, warning text, user confirmation and context is directly useful for the 15-day refund-or-refuse decision and for ADR.
- B2B batch opt-outs (80% and 67% at one bank) leave corporate treasury and AP flows with no VoP. This is exactly where AI agents (AP automation, invoice-processing agents) will be first to initiate payments, and where AI-generated invoices are rising.

### Gaps
- No primary EPC data on VoP match rates or on 2025–2026 APP/scam loss after VoP. The 2.2 billion checks and "one in three not clean" figures are secondary.
- Final PSR article numbers and the confirmed Official Journal date as of November 2026 were not verified.
- The content of the VoP rulebook v2.0 compensation process was not available.

## 3. Bank and regulator plans for AI-agent-initiated payments; missing controls

### Takeaway
In 2026, regulators moved from "existing rules apply" to explicitly studying agentic payments. The ECB is testing AI-agent payments in digital-euro pilots. HKMA launched Project SAGE (2 November 2026) with a Know Your Agent and delegated-authority focus. The Bank of England and FCA flagged consent for multiple transactions, liability, disputes and SCA. The Euro Banking Association asked for "human in command" checkpoints, bounded decision rights and end-to-end auditability. Spanish banks (Santander, BBVA, CaixaBank, Bankinter, Abanca) ran live agent payments with Mastercard and Visa under PSD2 with no specific rules. Recurring missing controls: SCA dynamic linking when amount and payee are unknown at delegation, proof that a transaction fell within the mandate, provenance of the beneficiary data, and dispute evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Euro Banking Association (ABE-EBA, the industry association, not the European Banking Authority), 10 June 2026 report "Supercharging embedded finance with agentic AI": it identified over 40 use cases and shortlisted five (cashflow optimiser, procurement/commerce optimiser, KYC/KYB onboarding guide, payment reconciliation assistant, product configuration manager). Its five success factors are real-time API access, end-to-end auditability, clear accountability and liability allocation, "human in command" checkpoints for critical decisions, and explicitly bounded decision rights for agents. Market priorities: interoperable standards, cross-industry digital identity for agentic systems, and safety principles aligned with the AI Act — [ABE-EBA press release PDF](https://www.abe-eba.eu//wp-content/uploads/2026/06/EBA_20260610_PR_OFWG_Agentic-AI_v1.0.pdf)
- European Banking Authority: a September 2025 note found that 55% of surveyed banks already use GPAI or agentic AI in consumer-facing processes — [EBA AI monitoring](https://www.eba.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-09/146b3558-d026-47bf-a872-f05e93ed30d2/Rising%20application%20of%20AI%20in%20EU%20banking%20and%20payments%20sector.pdf). A December 2025 factsheet found no significant contradictions between the AI Act and EU banking/payments law — [A&O Shearman](https://finreg.aoshearman.com/eba-factsheet-on-implications-of-eu-ai-act-for-banking-and-payments-sector). At the February 2026 EFIF meeting, participants raised SCA, fraud and liability concerns about agentic AI in payments — [ESMA EFIF summary](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/EFIF_SoCs_meeting_February_2026.pdf)
- ECB: a call opened around 28–29 September 2026 for the digital-euro innovation platform, explicitly including AI agents initiating or coordinating payments, with workshops in H1 2027 on AI-enabled payments, micropayments and machine-to-machine payments. A 5 November 2026 Cipollone speech summary says the new pilot wave (36 PSPs) will test electronic receipts, conditional payments and payments made by AI agents (secondary sources) — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/ecb-private-firms-ai-agents-digital-euro); [gncrypto](https://www.gncrypto.news/news/ecb-ai-agent-payments-digital-euro-trials/); [Lovguiden](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/den-europaeiske-centralbank/2026-11-05-ecb-digital-euro-pilot-in-2027-and-possible-first-issuance-in-2029)
- HKMA Project SAGE ("Securing Agentic Finance with Governance and Enablement") launched on 2 November 2026 with an Agentic Finance Expert Group. Its scope covers B2C, B2B and M2M payments (agentic commerce, procurement, treasury). It will study verification of agent identity and delegated authority (KYA), liability, consumer protection and interoperability. A discussion paper asks whether a central register of trusted agents or an industry KYA standard would help. No binding requirements yet — [HKMA press release](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261102-4/); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/hkma-launches-project-sage-to-support-agentic-finance-in-hong-kong)
- UK: Bank of England Deputy Governor Sarah Breeden (June 2026) named secure consent and authorisation to agents "especially for multiple transactions," dispute settlement, liability for erroneous or fraudulent transactions, and protocol fragmentation. She said "relying on a human in the loop for all agent actions is unlikely to be realistic." FCA CEO Nikhil Rathi said accountability must stay clear and agentic systems need human oversight. The Mills Review (July 2026) recommends an "agentic supervisory model" and a perimeter review. Proposals discussed include two-stage authorisation (mandate, then a per-transaction in-mandate check), time- and scope-limited agent credentials, merchant verification of agent authority, a new regulated agentic payment-initiation role, override rights (pause, reverse, cap), and extending APP reimbursement to prompt-injection-driven fraud — [Pinsent Masons, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)
- The FCA's March 2026 Payments Regulatory Priorities say it will consider, with HM Treasury, whether regulatory change is needed for agentic AI payments. The 19 October 2026 Rusu speech says Consumer Duty and SM&CR apply to AI and that autonomy raises the stakes for harm — [Payment Expert Mar 2026](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/03/25/fca-2026-payments-regulatory-priorities-report/); [Linklaters](https://techinsights.linklaters.com/post/102mo0a/uk-payments-priorities-for-2026-include-agentic-ai-and-stablecoins); [FCA speech](https://fca.org.uk/news/speeches/building-future-finance-age-ai); [Freshfields Oct 2026](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/briefings/2026/10/ai-in-financial-services-fca-good-and-poor-practice-and-the-road-to-agentic-finance)
- Spain: Santander and Mastercard reportedly completed Europe's first live AI-agent payment in March 2026. finReg360 notes there are no specific rules and PSD2 applies — [finReg360](https://finreg360.com/pagos-agenticos-cuando-quien-paga-es-un-agente-de-ia/). CaixaBank, BBVA, Bankinter and Abanca took part in pilots of fully agent-initiated transactions, which Latinia ties to Visa "Agentic Ready" (July 2026) — [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/?p=507977); [Latinia](https://latinia.com/en/resources/agentic-payments-spain-visa)
- Banco de España: I found no specific guidance on agentic payments. The spring 2026 Financial Stability Report box warns that AI changes the cyber-risk profile and that exploiting vulnerabilities in critical payment-system elements could disrupt operations — [BdE IEF 2026](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/Secciones/Publicaciones/InformesBoletinesRevistas/InformesEstabilidadFinancera/26/IEF_2026_1_Rec5_3.pdf)
- PSR/SCA structural issue: dynamic linking binds SCA to a known amount and payee, so when an agent's purchases are delegated before amount and payee are known, compliant SCA at execution is "structurally impossible." Possible routes are merchant-initiated transactions, transaction risk analysis exemptions and trusted beneficiaries — [Sopra Steria](https://www.soprasteria.com/insights/details/payment-services-regulation-psr-in-2026--the-state-of-play-for-banks). Agents cannot provide inherence factors, and when a user sets a budget instead of approving each payment, whether each payment counts as "authorised" is unclear — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)
- Industry protocols: Persona lists ACP, AP2, UCP and Visa TAP as the agentic-commerce protocols KYA plugs into — [Persona KYA](https://withpersona.com/lp/know-your-agent/). A Fenwick analysis (April 2026, cited secondhand) says the core unresolved question is who bears the loss when an agent exceeds its mandate — [e27](https://e27.co/from-kyc-to-kya-how-ai-agents-are-reshaping-payment-risk-20260813/)

### Inferences
- Missing controls repeated across regulators: (a) machine-checkable mandates (limits, scope, expiry) enforced per transaction, as in the BoE/UK "two-stage authorisation"; (b) evidence that the payee and amount came from a trusted source, not from injected content; (c) a human confirmation step that is risk-based, not per action, since Breeden says human-in-the-loop for everything is unrealistic; (d) portable, signed dispute evidence for the authorised/unauthorised decision and the PSR refund. KYA covers agent identity; card-network protocols (TAP, Agent Pay) cover agent recognition at merchants. A bank-side policy and evidence layer for account-to-account (SEPA Inst/VoP) agent payments looks under-served, especially in the EU where A2A is regulated by the IPR and PSR.
- The ECB testing "electronic receipts, conditional payments and AI-agent payments" together signals that signed receipts plus conditional release (a human hold) are on the central bank's own agenda. That alignment is a strong story for EU judges.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve primary ECB press releases or the full Breeden speech, and the text of the Euro Banking Association report itself is member-only.
- Specific technical gaps in Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay and Google AP2 (for example, whether AP2 "mandates" carry provenance of the beneficiary data) were not verified against specifications.
- No European Banking Authority (EU agency) statement dedicated to agentic payments was found for 2026.

## 4. Prompt-injection and data-exfiltration incidents with browser/computer-use agents that have financial consequences (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
There are well-documented attack demonstrations and in-the-wild payloads aimed at moving money (Brave and LayerX against Perplexity Comet in 2025; Unit 42 and Forcepoint finding live pages instructing agents to send $5,000 via PayPal in 2026). There are no verified, quantified bank-loss cases from AI browser agents. Claims of $500K or $600K in losses come from weak secondary sources.

### Cited Findings
- Brave (August 2025) disclosed indirect prompt injection in Perplexity's Comet. Hidden page content (for example, a Reddit comment) could make the agent act inside the user's logged-in sessions, including navigating to accounts and exfiltrating data to an attacker server. A later Brave finding (October 2025) involved hidden text in screenshots being read by OCR and treated as instructions — [Brave blog](https://brave.com/blog/comet-prompt-injection/)
- LayerX's "CometJacking" (disclosed 27–28 August 2025) put a payload in a URL query string, which Comet treated as a prompt, to pull data from connected Gmail and Calendar — [CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/comet-ai-browser-vulnerable-to-full-data-exfiltration-via-malicious-urls); [Aviatrix summary](https://aviatrix.ai/threat-research-center/cometjacking-attack-2025-perplexity-comet-browser-prompt-injection/). The Aviatrix page also claims a CVE, a CVSS score and a ~$500K loss that I could not verify; treat them as unverified.
- Palo Alto Unit 42 documented web-based indirect prompt injection observed in the wild, including a hidden prompt directing an agent to a legitimate payment page with an attacker-controlled account and attempting to send $5,000 — [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/ai-agent-prompt-injection/)
- Forcepoint X-Labs (April 2026) found ten verified injection payloads on live sites, including one with a PayPal.me link, a fixed $5,000 amount and step-by-step transfer instructions for agents (via secondary summary) — [technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/10/17/ai-agent-prompt-injection-evidence/); [RAXE-2026-016](https://raxe.ai/labs/advisories/RAXE-2026-016)
- Google reported a 32% relative increase in malicious indirect-injection content between November 2025 and February 2026, while noting most attacks were unsophisticated (secondary) — [technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/10/17/ai-agent-prompt-injection-evidence/)
- RAXE Labs rates web-based indirect injection as high impact, including direct financial loss (unauthorised purchases, forced donations) — [RAXE-2026-016](https://raxe.ai/labs/advisories/RAXE-2026-016)
- UK analysis explicitly cites prompt injection and manipulated product listings as routes to agent-driven fraud, and suggests extending APP reimbursement or holding developers or merchants liable — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)

### Inferences
- The concrete attack a bank layer must stop is "an untrusted web, email or document token becomes the beneficiary IBAN, amount or recipient name of a payment." That is an information-flow-control problem. Taint-tracking the payee fields, so that a payment whose IBAN derives from untrusted content requires an oracle check (VoP plus the customer's known-beneficiary list) or a human hold, maps directly to the documented $5,000 PayPal payloads.
- Since no verified loss case exists, a pitch should frame this as documented in-the-wild attempts plus regulator concern, not as "$X lost."

### Gaps
- No primary-source, quantified financial-loss incident involving a browser or computer-use agent and a bank or PSP was found.
- The current patch status of Comet and of other agentic browsers was not verified. The report of OpenAI retiring ChatGPT Atlas on 9 August 2026 comes from a single weak source and is not relied on.

## 5. Where a layer with tokenized data, provenance (information-flow control), oracle, human hold and signed receipts gives unique value; candidate products scored against the hackathon rubric

### Takeaway
The strongest fit is a bank- or PSP-side "agent payment guard" for SEPA Instant. It tokenizes payee data, tracks where each field came from, checks it against oracles (VoP, the customer's known beneficiaries, mule and fraud feeds), applies a risk-based human hold delivered by voice in natural language, and issues a signed receipt that serves as evidence under the PSR. It answers gaps that regulators named (mandates, provenance, human-in-command, disputes) and that existing KYA and AML-agent vendors do not cover.

### Cited Findings (anchors for value claims)
- Regulators and industry ask for bounded decision rights, end-to-end auditability, human-in-command checkpoints and clear liability — [ABE-EBA](https://www.abe-eba.eu//wp-content/uploads/2026/06/EBA_20260610_PR_OFWG_Agentic-AI_v1.0.pdf); and for two-stage authorisation, scope-limited credentials and override rights — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)
- The PSR puts the burden of proof on PSPs, gives them a 15-business-day refund-or-refuse window, and stops treating SCA as proof of negligence — [Sopra Steria](https://www.soprasteria.com/insights/details/payment-services-regulation-psr-in-2026--the-state-of-play-for-banks)
- VoP is advisory only, and liability shifts when a warning is overridden. v2.0 adds an inquiry and compensation process — [Fintech Weekly](https://www.fintechweekly.com/magazine/articles/verification-of-payee-one-year-instant-payments-2026)
- The ECB's next pilot tests electronic receipts, conditional payments and AI-agent payments — [Lovguiden](https://www.lovguiden.dk/en/det-offentlige/den-europaeiske-centralbank/2026-11-05-ecb-digital-euro-pilot-in-2027-and-possible-first-issuance-in-2029)
- In-the-wild injection payloads target payments with fixed amounts and attacker accounts — [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/ai-agent-prompt-injection/)
- DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, applicable since 17 January 2025) requires ICT risk management, incident reporting and third-party risk oversight, which covers AI model providers used by banks — [EUR-Lex DORA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj). The UK says the critical-third-party regime is "more important than ever" as firms rely on AI providers — [Pinsent Masons](https://www.pinsentmasons.com/out-law/analysis/what-new-rules-agentic-ai-payments-look-like)
- AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): under the Digital Omnibus provisional deal (7 May 2026; Parliament vote on 16 June 2026), Annex III high-risk obligations, including credit scoring, move from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027, and product-embedded systems to 2 August 2028. Final Official Journal status was not confirmed in this research — [Sidley](https://datamatters.sidley.com/2026/06/22/eu-lawmakers-reach-provisional-agreement-to-delay-key-eu-ai-act-obligations/); [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/); [IDTechWire](https://idtechwire.com/eu-moves-to-delay-high-risk-ai-act-compliance-to-december-2027/); [EUR-Lex AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj). Fraud-detection AI is carved out of the Annex III credit-scoring category in the AI Act text (Annex III point 5(b)); this is my reading of the Act, not re-verified here.

### Inferences: candidate products and rubric scoring
Rubric weights: innovation/viability 30%, implementation 30%, frontier AI language+audio measurable impact 15%, UX/accessibility 15%, presentation 10%. Scores are out of 10 and are my judgement; the weighted totals are out of 10.

1. **"Agent Payment Guard" for SEPA Instant/VoP (bank or PSP side).** A customer's agent (or a TPP under PSD3) proposes a transfer. The layer tokenizes the IBAN and name, tags each field's provenance (user-typed, saved beneficiary, extracted from email/web/PDF/SMS), checks oracles (VoP result, known beneficiaries, mandate limits, mule lists), and holds the payment when tainted data reaches a payee field or a mandate bound is exceeded. The hold is a voice or plain-language confirmation in Spanish or English that explains the risk ("this IBAN came from an email you received 3 minutes ago; VoP says close match"). It ends with a signed receipt (VoP outcome, warning shown, user response, mandate ID, provenance graph) usable for PSR refund decisions and ADR. Innovation/viability 9 (fills the named gaps: mandate, provenance, human-in-command, dispute evidence; EU-specific and timely), implementation 8 (VoP can be mocked against the EPC response codes; IFC over a few fields is tractable in a hackathon), AI language+audio 8 (voice explanation and confirmation, scam-call transcript analysis, measurable as false-hold rate and scam-catch rate on a synthetic set), UX/accessibility 8 (voice-first confirmation helps elderly and visually impaired users, a key scam demographic), presentation 9 (a live demo of an injected "$5,000 PayPal" page being stopped is vivid). **Weighted ≈ 8.4.**
2. **"Dispute Evidence Pack" for the PSR impersonation refund.** Signed receipts plus an LLM that assembles the 15-day refund-or-refuse file and the customer-facing written justification. Innovation 7, implementation 8, AI 7, UX 6, presentation 7. **≈ 7.2.** Stronger as a feature of #1 than on its own.
3. **B2B "AP agent firewall" for opted-out batches.** It enforces VoP-like checks and provenance on invoice-derived IBANs inside ERP/AP agents, covering the 80% batch opt-out gap and AI-written invoice fraud. Innovation 8, implementation 7 (needs ERP mock), AI 6 (document extraction, less audio), UX 6, presentation 7. **≈ 7.0.**
4. **Voice "scam interrupter" at payment time.** It listens, with consent, during a call or analyses the customer's spoken description of why they are paying, then flags safe-account, investment or romance scripts that VoP cannot catch. Innovation 7 (BeeSafe, Rulebase and Charm are nearby competitors), implementation 7, AI 9, UX 8, presentation 8. **≈ 7.5.** Combines well with #1 as the human-hold channel.
5. **AML alert-triage agent.** Innovation 3 (saturated: Bretton, Arva, Diligent, Fenrock, Unit21, Sardine), implementation 8, AI 6, UX 5, presentation 6. **≈ 5.4.** Not recommended.

- Recommended framing: #1 as the core, with #4's voice hold as the human-in-command channel and #2's evidence pack as the output. Position it as "VoP tells you the name matches; we tell you whether the payee came from someone you should trust, and we prove it later." That differs from KYA vendors (agent identity), AML agents (back office) and card-network agent protocols (merchant side).
- Measurable impact metrics for the 15% criterion: detection rate on a synthetic set of injected and scam payment attempts versus a VoP-only baseline, false-hold rate on legitimate payments, time to confirm by voice versus app, and completeness of the evidence pack against a PSR refund checklist.

### Gaps
- No market data on bank willingness to pay, or on how many EU banks already plan agent-payment controls.
- Whether Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay or AP2 already carry field-level provenance or signed mandates comparable to #1 was not verified; this needs checking before claiming uniqueness.
- The legal weight of a vendor-signed receipt as PSR or ADR evidence is untested.
