# Sponsors (MyInvestor, GPTadvisor) and painful Spanish consumer-finance procedures: inheritances, complaints, transfers, execution vs advice

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. 17 tool calls. Figures marked (UNCERTAIN) come from secondary sources or sources that conflict.

## 1. What MyInvestor and GPTadvisor are, and what they would value

### Takeaway
MyInvestor is a fast-growing low-cost Spanish neobank (more than 1M clients, about EUR 19.2bn business volume), and investment is now its core business. GPTadvisor is a profitable, bootstrapped B2B wealthtech from Valencia that builds agentic AI for banks and advisors (built on MCP and Claude, in the CNMV sandbox). Together they launched an MCP integration that lets ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity query MyInvestor's catalogue and *start* a purchase, but the user has to *finish* it in MyInvestor's app "por seguridad". That missing step from "AI starts it" to "AI finishes it, safely" is the gap both sponsors care about.

### Inferences
- Both sponsors already use MCP, Claude and human approval. A project that is "another chatbot that recommends funds" will look like work they already shipped. A project that closes their open gap will land better: a secure, auditable *last mile* (consent, human hold, signed execution evidence), or a way to bring *external* assets in (transfers of funds and pensions from other banks, inheritances that release money).
- MyInvestor makes money from AUM inflows (EUR 400M a month). Anything that turns money stuck at legacy banks into easier transfers (fund and pension transfers, unblocked inherited accounts) lines up directly with its growth KPI.
- GPTadvisor sells to *advisors and institutions*. It would value a reusable control layer (provenance, policy, audit) that a regulated institution could deploy, more than a pure B2C app.

### Gaps
- No public MyInvestor open API for third parties, beyond the MCP server, was found. Whether its MCP server is publicly reachable or open to hackathon teams was not confirmed.
- No stated "pain points" from sponsor staff were found, beyond security as the reason orders are not executed in the chat.
- GPTadvisor headcount and ARR are not officially confirmed.

## 2. Inheritances (herencias / testamentarías) in Spain

### Takeaway
About 441,000 deaths a year (2025). Each one sets off a sequential, multi-portal procedure with legal deadlines: Ministry of Justice registries, the notary, each bank (accounts blocked), insurers, the regional tax portal (modelo 650 within 6 months), municipal plusvalía, and the land registry. Inheritance is the 5th most common subject of Banco de España complaints. Existing help is either human-heavy flat-fee legaltech (Heritae about EUR 999–2,999) or bank-by-bank in-house services (Bankinter EUR 85, BBVA and CaixaBank online forms). No one *orchestrates across all the institutions*.

### Cited Findings
- 441,270 deaths in Spain in 2025 (provisional, cause-of-death statistic); a separate monthly estimate gives 446,982 — [INE](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/pEDCM2025.htm); [INE EMN 2025](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/EDES_EMN2025.htm)
- Certificado de últimas voluntades: issued by the Registro General de Actos de Última Voluntad (Ministry of Justice). It shows whether there is a will and before which notary. It can be requested 15 business days after death, online, in person or by post, and needs the death certificate. It is issued within about 10 business days — [administracion.gob.es](https://administracion.gob.es/pag_Home/Tu-espacio-europeo/derechos-obligaciones/ciudadanos/familia/sucesiones/info-general.html); [INEAF](https://www.ineaf.es/tribuna/certificado-ultimas-voluntades/)
- Old data point: in one July more than 15,000 requests came in and about half were resolved automatically through the sede electrónica (year predates 2025) — [elderecho.com](https://elderecho.com/justicia-acelera-la-expedicion-automatica-de-certificados-de-ultimas-voluntades)
- Certificado del Registro de Contratos de Seguros de Cobertura de Fallecimiento: required in the AEAT modelo 650 document list. Sources disagree on timing (requestable from 15 days after death and issued within 7 days, vs other claims) (UNCERTAIN) — [AEAT modelo 650 documentation](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/declaraciones-informativas-otros-impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-sucesiones-donaciones/adquisiciones-mortis-causa-modelo-650/documentacion-aportar.html)
- Impuesto de Sucesiones (modelo 650): **6 months from death**, extendable 6 more months on request (generally within the first 5 months). Filed by each heir in the region (CCAA) where the deceased lived, and rules vary by region — [AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/declaraciones-informativas-otros-impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-sucesiones-donaciones/adquisiciones-mortis-causa-modelo-650/documentacion-aportar.html); [AEAT prórroga tutorial (non-residents)](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Procedimiento_ayuda/G702/Tutoriales/AyudaCumplimentFormularioSolicitudProrogaNR.pdf); [TaxDown](https://taxdown.es/modelos-hacienda/modelo-650-sucesiones)
- Insurers usually require proof that the inheritance tax was paid before they pay out the death benefit (modelo 652 exists for life insurance in some regions) — [Diari de Tarragona](https://www.diaridetarragona.com/economia/hacienda/266463/errores-comunes-herencias-seguros-vida-espana.html); [Canarias modelo 652](https://www3.gobiernodecanarias.org/tributos/atc/w/modelo-652)
- Bank steps (OCU): notify the bank; find out which accounts exist ("Hacienda is the only place with that information"); accounts held only by the deceased are blocked; ask for a balance certificate as of the date of death (must be free); deed of acceptance and partition plus proof of tax payment; then release. Some banks make heirs sign a request that includes a paid "testamentaría" service. The Banco de España considers document checking to be in the bank's own interest, so a fee is justified only for a genuine advisory service. At least one free way to withdraw the money must exist. Inheritances are the **5th** most common subject of BdE complaints. If the SAC does not answer within 1 month, or answers against you, you can go to the BdE — [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/herencia/consejos/cuenta-fallecido)
- Bank services: Bankinter charges EUR 85 for testamentaría management; BBVA lets clients start it online (Mis trámites > Tramitación de herencias); CaixaBank and Santander have online testamentaría services — [Bankinter](https://www.bankinter.com/banca/ayuda/tramite-testamento-heredero-cuentas); [BBVA](https://www.bbva.es/general/finanzas-vistazo/cuentas/gestiones-bancarias-herencia-fallecimiento/index.jsp); [CaixaBank](https://www.caixabank.es/particular/general/testamentaria.html); [Santander](https://www.bancosantander.es/particulares/banca-online/servicios-testamentarias/)
- Copy of the will about EUR 30–60 (UNCERTAIN, secondary source) — [GovEasy](https://es.goveasy.eu/blog/herencia-tramites-impuestos-espana-2026)
- Startups: **Heritae** (Madrid, about 2020; full flat-fee handling from certificates to legal and tax advice; prices reported as EUR 999 + VAT, or EUR 2,999 incl. VAT for estates under EUR 200k (UNCERTAIN, the two figures conflict)); **Testamenta** (Sabadell, 2012, online wills and inheritances with notaries); **Everly** (Seville, online wills and legacy planning); **Reclamador** (online inheritance handling with lawyers); Legálitas–CaixaBank free will; EU project LiV (blockchain for inheritances) — [El Referente/Heritae](https://elreferente.es/inversiones/nace-heritae-startup-espanola-gestiona-manera-integral-herencias/); [El Referente directory](https://elreferente.es/directorio/heritae); [El Referente/Testamenta](https://elreferente.es/directorio/testamenta/); [Legálitas](https://www.legalitas.com/actualidad/alianza-caixabank-testamentos); [CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101113879/es)
- Unclaimed money: accounts with no movement for **20 years** pass to the State (art. 18 Ley 33/2003). Banks report them to Hacienda in Q1 each year. Cash taken in: EUR 18.33M in 2024 (DG Patrimonio memoria); EUR 199.7M between 2018 and 2023; peak EUR 62.65M in 2020. Hacienda does not publish which accounts or entities are affected — [BdE Cliente Bancario](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/cuentas-bancarias-abandonadas.html); [The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/2025-10-01/hacienda-cuentas-abandonadas-ingreso-200-millones-2018/); [El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/economia/20250825/estado-ingresa-283-millones-euros-cuentas-bancarias-estas-son-afectadas_328123.html)

### Inferences
- Order of operations and dependencies: death certificate, then 15 business days, then últimas voluntades and seguros certificates, then will copy from the notary, then balance certificates from every bank, insurer and fund manager, then estate inventory and valuation, then deed at the notary, then modelo 650 per heir (6-month clock), then municipal plusvalía, then each bank releases funds, the insurers pay, and title changes at the land registry and DGT. That is about 6 or more distinct institutions with portals or forms, each waiting on the previous step: an ideal *agent orchestration* problem with deadlines.
- The "which accounts did the deceased have?" problem is real (OCU: only Hacienda knows). An agent that holds the heir's tokenized, consented access and sends balance-certificate requests to every candidate bank, with provenance on every number feeding the modelo 650, adds unique value.
- Sponsor fit: MyInvestor gains when inherited money is released and reinvested; it can also be the *receiving* institution. A strong demo: "the heir's 6-month clock, live dashboard, every document with provenance, human hold before signing at the notary or filing the 650, signed audit log for the gestor/notary".
- Audio and voice matter here: heirs are often elderly or grieving. A voice interface that explains each step in plain Spanish and calls or dictates requests scores on accessibility and the 15% frontier-AI criterion.
- Legal risk: filing the 650 or signing deeds needs the heir's own certificate or Cl@ve, or a professional with representation. The agent should *prepare* and *pre-fill* and leave the human to sign, which is exactly the human-hold design.

### Gaps
- No official count of certificados de últimas voluntades issued in 2025, and no count of inheritances processed per year by banks.
- No reliable average total time or cost of an inheritance (anecdotally months); typical notary and gestoría fees not sourced.
- Current funding and size of Heritae, Testamenta and Everly not found. Tecnotramit was not verified as active in inheritances.
- Municipal plusvalía deadline (generally 6 months for death, extendable) not sourced in this round.

## 3. Banking and consumer complaints (reclamaciones)

### Takeaway
More than 1M complaints a year go to banks' customer service departments (SAC), and 58% are resolved against the customer. Only about 31,000 escalate to the Banco de España, and only a third of those are admitted. When admitted, the customer wins about half the time. Fraud (cards and transfers) is the #1 topic (30%) and growing. The funnel loses most people between the SAC "no" and a well-built BdE case: an agent can assemble the evidence and file it.

### Cited Findings
- BdE Memoria 2025 (published 21 Jul 2026): 30,970 complaints received (−44.8%); 10,856 admitted; 35,735 consultations; two thirds filed online. Admitted outcomes: 39% for the bank, 33% settled, 20% report favourable to the claimant, 8% no ruling. Rectification index 79%. Record EUR 6.8M refunded (+34%), average EUR 625 per admitted complaint — [BdE Cliente Bancario blog](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/servicio-de-reclamaciones-del-banco-de-espana-que-es-y-que-datos-deja-la-memoria-de-2025.html); [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/); [BdE MSR2025 Cap1](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/Secciones/Publicaciones/PublicacionesAnuales/MemoriaServicioReclamaciones/25/MSR2025_Cap1.pdf)
- Banks' own SACs handled 1,061,153 direct complaints in 2025 (−49%); 58% resolved against the customer; 7% of those escalated to the BdE — [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/); [BdE Síntesis](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/Secciones/Publicaciones/PublicacionesAnuales/MemoriaServicioReclamaciones/25/Sintesis_de_la_Memoria.pdf)
- Topics: fraud 9,179 (30%, +18%), of which 66% cards and 34% transfers; mortgage-expense claims 3,019 (10%, −90%); fees about 13% of admitted complaints. By product: cards 31.3%, accounts/transfers/direct debits 29.1%, mortgages 22.1%, personal loans 7.9% — [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/); [BdE blog](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/servicio-de-reclamaciones-del-banco-de-espana-que-es-y-que-datos-deja-la-memoria-de-2025.html)
- Most complained-about banks: CaixaBank 6,156, BBVA 5,700, Santander 4,229, ING 1,853, Sabadell 1,651, Bankinter 1,442 — [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/)
- Trend: complaints up more than 50% year on year in Jan–Apr 2026; more than 20,500 in H1 2026; about 40,000 projected for 2026 — [BdE blog](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/servicio-de-reclamaciones-del-banco-de-espana-que-es-y-que-datos-deja-la-memoria-de-2025.html); [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/)
- 2024 was a record 56,000+, driven by the Supreme Court ruling 857/2024 on mortgage expenses — [Forbes](https://forbes.es/economia/773200/el-banco-de-espana-registra-un-record-de-56-000-reclamaciones-bancarias-en-2024/); [BdE blog 2024](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/que-nos-dicen-mas-de-56000-reclamaciones-y-54000-consultas-sobre-nuestra-relacion-con-los-bancos-en-2024.html?blog=%2Fwebcb%2FBlog)
- Procedure: complain first to the SAC or Defensor del Cliente; if there is no answer within 1 month or it is unfavourable, go to the BdE — [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/herencia/consejos/cuenta-fallecido)
- Fraud law: the EU PSD revision compromise (end of 2025) would shift more fraud liability to banks from 2028 unless they apply effective prevention. Current rules do "not protect the user who was manipulated into authorising" the payment (Pablo Vega, Roams) — [Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/)
- Reclamador: reported revolving-card claims up 125% in H1 2025 — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/mercados/economia--las-reclamaciones-a-bancos-por-tarjetas-revolving-aumentan-un-125-hasta-junio-segun-reclamadores--20449278.html)

### Inferences
- The volume (1M at the SAC vs 31k at the BdE) and the 58% rejection rate at the SAC suggest many valid complaints are abandoned. A "complaint agent" that pulls transaction evidence from the bank portal (tokenized), drafts the SAC complaint citing the regulation, tracks the 1-month clock and escalates to the BdE portal with a full dossier has measurable impact: complaints filed, time saved, EUR recovered.
- Weaker sponsor fit: MyInvestor would be the *defendant*, not the beneficiary, and incumbents (Reclamador, lawyers on success fees) already exist. Better as a feature (for example "inheritance fee charged unduly, file a complaint") than as the core.
- Fraud-victim angle: voice is well suited to elderly victims, but the agent operating the victim's compromised bank account raises the security bar.

### Gaps
- BdE and CNMV legal response deadlines (90 days, etc.) and whether BdE reports are binding were not confirmed in this round. CNMV and DGSFP 2025 complaint volumes not gathered.
- Status of the Spanish "Autoridad de Defensa del Cliente Financiero" law as of Nov 2026 not verified.
- Reclamador's own volumes, success rates and fees not found.

## 4. Transfers and portability (cambio de banco, fund and pension transfers, scattered savings)

### Takeaway
Spain has legally fast, tax-neutral rails (account switching in 13 business days, free; fund transfers 5–8 business days; pension plan transfers 5–7 business days). They are started at the *destination* entity, but the user must still gather every origin account, ISIN, plan and recurring payment by hand across many portals. This is a direct growth lever for MyInvestor (bringing in external AUM).

### Cited Findings
- Payment account switching (RDL 19/2017, Orden ECE/228/2019): banks must cooperate. The whole process takes at most 13 business days: the new bank asks the old one within 2 business days, the old one sends the list of direct debits and recurring transfers within 5, the new one activates them within 5. Free of charge; all holders must sign. Sources disagree on the minimum start date — [BOE RDL 19/2017](https://boe.es/buscar/pdf/2017/BOE-A-2017-13644-consolidado.pdf); [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/cuenta-bancaria/noticias/traspaso-cuentas-entidades); [Bankinter](https://www.bankinter.com/banca/cuentas-tarjetas/cuentas/trasladar-cambiar-cuenta-bancaria-gratis); [wwwhatsnew, Oct 2026](https://wwwhatsnew.com/2026/10/27/cambiar-de-banco-traslado-cuenta-recibos-plazos/)
- Fund transfers: requested at the destination entity. At most 5 business days within the same manager or distributor; about 8 business days between different entities (longer if other institutions are involved). Liquidity windows can delay it — [CNMV ficha traspasos](https://www.cnmv.es/DocPortal/Publicaciones/Fichas/TraspasosEntreFondosDeInversion.pdf); [CNMV GR15](https://internet.cnmv.es/DocPortal/Publicaciones/Fichas/GR15_Traspasos_entre_fondos.pdf)
- Pension plan transfers: at most 7 business days between different pension managers (RD 304/2004). Workplace plans are generally not transferable unless the employment ends — [Fundación MAPFRE](https://documentacion.fundacionmapfre.org/documentacion/media/group/1053213.do); [BBVA Mi Jubilación](https://www.bbvamijubilacion.es/blog/rescate-o-traspaso-de-planes-de-pensiones-cuales-son-los-plazos-legales/)
- MyInvestor sells index pension plans and funds that are eligible for transfers — [MyInvestor planes indexados](https://myinvestor.es/landings/planes-pensiones/indexados/global/)
- BBVA has an "app traspasos" for pension plans (an incumbent doing transfer intake digitally) — [BBVA](https://www.bbva.es/personas/productos/planes-de-pensiones/app-traspasos.html)

### Inferences
- The pain is not the legal rail but *discovery and data entry*: finding every fund and plan, its ISIN, the origin entity's account and the holder data, often from PDFs and statements across 3–5 portals. An agent that reads statements and portals (tokenized), builds a "consolidation map" with provenance and pre-fills MyInvestor transfer orders, with a human hold before signing, is easy to demo and maps straight onto the sponsor's KPI (inflows).
- It is "ejecuta, no aconseja"-friendly: moving the user's own holdings to the same or a user-chosen product is order reception and transmission, not advice, if the agent does not recommend the destination product.

### Gaps
- No Spain-specific data on how many fund or pension transfers happen per year (Inverco has the data but it was not pulled) or on the failure and delay rates of transfers.
- No startup found that specifically aggregates and consolidates scattered Spanish savings across entities (beyond PSD2 aggregators, not researched here).

## 5. Execution vs advice: what an AI agent may legally do (MiFID II / CNMV)

### Takeaway
The CNMV is publicly warning about AI investing without human supervision and plans AI guidance for 2026. The sponsors' live products already draw the line at "AI informs or starts it, the human finishes it and the advisor approves". An agent that *executes only what the user explicitly ordered*, with no recommendation, a human hold and a signed audit trail, sits in the safer zone and directly answers the CNMV's stated concern.

### Cited Findings
- June 2026: the CNMV warned investors about using generative AI without human supervision for stock-market decisions ("errores impresionantes", hallucinations, false sense of security) — [Funds Society](https://www.fundssociety.com/es/noticias/normativa/el-uso-de-la-ia-sin-supervision-humana-en-decisiones-de-inversion-presenta-errores-impresionantes/); [Forbes](https://forbes.es/economia/904499/la-cnmv-alerta-de-que-el-uso-de-la-ia-en-inversion-presenta-fallos-errores-y-alucinaciones/); [Merca2](https://www.merca2.es/2026/06/07/cnmv-alerta-ia-invertir-bolsa-2391693/)
- CNMV Plan de Actividades 2026 includes guides on online financial fraud, the **use of AI**, and crypto-fraud — [CNMV PDA2026](https://www.cnmv.es/DocPortal/Publicaciones/PlanActividad/PDA2026.pdf); [Finreg360](https://finreg360.com/alerta/la-cnmv-presenta-su-plan-de-actividades-para-2026/)
- CNMV president Carlos San Basilio: the CNMV has built tools to detect unauthorised advice on social networks and digital platforms — [Última Hora](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2026/05/07/2624843/san-basilio-reivindica-palma-papel-clave-del-asesor-financiero.html)

### Inferences
- Under MiFID II, a *personal recommendation* is advice (it needs authorisation and a suitability test). Receiving and transmitting orders that the client *decided* is a different service (appropriateness test for complex products). The design principle "ejecuta, no aconseja" means: the agent never picks the product; it executes the user's stated instruction, shows provenance for every data point, and holds for explicit confirmation. This is an inference from general MiFID structure; no specific 2026 CNMV text on AI agents was found.
- The "signed audit log" plus human hold also matches EU AI Act and DORA themes that GPTadvisor cites.

### Gaps
- No specific CNMV or ESMA 2026 guidance on AI agents *executing* orders was found (ESMA's May 2024 statement on AI in investment services was not fetched). The CNMV AI guide named in the PDA 2026 may not be published yet.
- Spanish rules on the legal value of an agent acting with the user's credentials (PSD2 SCA, access to Cl@ve) not researched.
