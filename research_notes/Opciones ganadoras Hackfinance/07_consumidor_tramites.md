# Consumer money procedures in Spain (2025-2026): where people lose money or time, and agent products that operate institutions' websites and phone lines

Research date: 10 Nov 2026. 19 web searches, plus the two earlier notes it builds on (`Producto ganador Hackfinance/inversion_herencias_reclamaciones.md` and `voz_finanzas_hackathons.md`). Figures marked (UNCERTAIN) come from secondary or commercial sources, or from sources that conflict. Scores and concepts are the researcher's judgement, not sourced facts.

## 1. How much money is left on the table in Spain

### Takeaway
The largest documented gap is in public aid that people are entitled to but never claim. AIReF estimates that 56% of households eligible for the IMV don't apply, and 72% don't claim the child supplement (CAPI). The bono social eléctrico reaches 1.73M households, while 3.5M more might qualify. Regional IRPF deductions are badly under-used: Funcas finds taxpayers claim less than 30% of the maximum. Banking money is smaller but concrete. The BdE got €6.8M back for complainants in 2025, about €625 per admitted complaint. Dormant accounts lost to the State total about €200M between 2018 and 2023.

### Cited Findings
**Unclaimed public aid**
- IMV non-take-up (AIReF): 57% in 2021 (about 400,000 eligible households had not applied), 58% in 2022 and 56% in 2023. In 2023, 36% of potential beneficiaries were receiving it (342,856 households). About 5 points of non-take-up are explained by households on regional minimum incomes, and 53% of non-take-up households receive unemployment benefits — [Noticias de Navarra / AIReF 2024](https://www.noticiasdenavarra.com/economia/2024/07/10/airef-calcula-36-posibles-beneficiarios-8463346.html); [The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/2024-07-10/airef-beneficiarios-ingreso-minimo-2023/); [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/economia/macroeconomia/airef-fracaso-ingreso-minimo-vital.html)
- In 2024 the IMV reached 42% of potentially eligible households and the CAPI (Complemento de Ayuda para la Infancia) only 23%. AIReF puts CAPI non-take-up at about 72%. CAPI households grew 34% to 452,234. AIReF proposes granting the IMV automatically (de oficio); CAPI is already granted automatically to IMV recipients but must be applied for by other low-income families with children — [El Salto](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/renta-basica/55percent-hogares-podrian-recibir-imv-no-lo-han-solicitado-segun-airef); [Bolsamanía/Europa Press](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/economia/economia--airef-constata-que-el-imv-llega-al-20-de-hogares-en-pobreza-pero-advierte-de-que-podria-beneficiar-al-50--21607160.html); [BBVA Mi Jubilación](https://www.bbvamijubilacion.es/blog/el-nuevo-complemento-a-la-infancia-para-los-perceptores-del-ingreso-minimo-vital-y-otras-rentas-bajas)
- AIReF: the IMV reaches 20% of households in poverty but could reach 50% — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/economia/economia--airef-constata-que-el-imv-llega-al-20-de-hogares-en-pobreza-pero-advierte-de-que-podria-beneficiar-al-50--21607160.html)
- Bono social eléctrico: 1,734,098 beneficiaries in June 2026, up 2.41% year on year (MITECO data via Selectra). Extraordinary discounts are 42.5% for vulnerable and 57.5% for severely vulnerable households until 31 Dec 2026. The CNMC puts the 2026 cost at €513M for 1.83M supply points — [Selectra cifras jun 2026](https://selectra.es/energia/info/bono-social/beneficiarios); [MITECO datos](https://www.miteco.gob.es/en/energia/energia-electrica/bono-social/datos-beneficiarios-bono-social.html); [Merca2](https://www.merca2.es/2026/05/27/cnmc-recalcula-bono-social-electrico-2026-2385118/); [Madrid.es](https://www.madrid.es/UnidadesDescentralizadas/Consumo/Promocionales/Nuevas%20medidas%20suministros%20energ%C3%A9ticos/ficheros/nuevas_medidas_energ%C3%ADa_jun26.pdf)
- Bono social non-take-up (estimates differ by method): about 3.5M households might be entitled but don't receive it ([Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/economia/energia/fiasco-bono-social-luz-35-millones-hogares-no-reciben-complejidad.html)); "half of potential beneficiaries have not applied" ([OCU](https://www.ocu.org/vivienda-y-energia/gas-luz/noticias/bono-social-conseguirlo-conservarlo)); it reaches only a quarter of potential beneficiaries (EsadeEcPol/Oxfam, 2024) ([El Debate](https://www.eldebate.com/economia/20240912/pinchazo-bono-electrico-apenas-llega-cuarta-parte-potenciales-beneficiarios_226696.html); [EsadeEcPol](https://www.esade.edu/ecpol/en/publications/llega-el-bono-social-electrico-a-quien-lo-necesita-analisis-de-las-tasas-de-cobertura-y-las-caracteristicas-del-hogar/))
- The Estrategia Nacional contra la Pobreza Energética 2026-2030 (Feb 2026) changes eligibility for the bono social eléctrico and térmico, adding an income criterion for large families — [MITECO](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/febrero/el-gobierno-aprueba-la-estrategia-nacional-contra-la-pobreza-ene.html); [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/empresas/economia--aprobada-la-estrategia-para-la-pobreza-energetica-que-incluye-un-criterio-de-renta-para-familias-numerosas--21726146.html)

**Missed IRPF deductions**

**Dormant accounts** (from the previous note)
- After 20 years without activity, accounts pass to the State (art. 18 Ley 33/2003). €18.33M in 2024; €199.7M in 2018-2023. Hacienda does not publish which accounts are affected — [BdE](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/cuentas-bancarias-abandonadas.html); [The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/2025-10-01/hacienda-cuentas-abandonadas-ingreso-200-millones-2018/)

**Linked insurance and subscriptions**
- Bank-sold insurance policies are often more expensive than the open market, especially financed single-premium life policies. If an outside policy meets the bank's conditions, the bank must accept it and keep the rate discount. OCU calls the market captive because consumers don't know they can refuse — [HelpMyCash](https://www.helpmycash.com/blog/los-seguros-vinculados-a-la-hipoteca-no-son-obligatorios/); [OCU hipotecas y seguros](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/hipotecas/informe/hipotecas-y-seguros); [Euribor.com.es/OCU](https://www.euribor.com.es/2022/07/11/la-ocu-alerta-sobre-los-seguros-al-contratar-la-hipopteca/?amp=1)
- (UNCERTAIN, single commercial source) Dropping a policy linked to the mortgage usually raises the spread by 0.10-0.60 points — [iAhorro](https://www.iahorro.com/blog/educacion-financiera/cancelar-seguro-vinculado-hipoteca)
- When a mortgage is repaid early, the linked insurance ends and the unused premium should be refunded (extorno). The Supreme Court held abusive a clause that imposed a financed single-premium policy with an insurer from the bank's group: refund with interest, minus the part already consumed — [Selectra](https://selectra.es/seguros/noticias/hipoteca-seguro-2026-banco-no-puede-imponer); [Finanzas/Finantres](https://finantresnoticias.com/bancos/supremo-primas-unicas-hipotecas-catalanes/)
- Subscriptions: Spanish households spend more than €70 a month on internet and entertainment platforms (CNMC, via a secondary source). Streaming prices rose 81.7% in 2015-2025, against 18.5% CPI. Europeans spend almost €700 a year on subscriptions (Bango 2024) — [Diari de Tarragona](https://www.diaridetarragona.com/economia/241062/coste-oculto-comodidad-tecnologia-multiplica-pequenos-gastos.html); [Menorca.info](https://www.menorca.info/actualidad/tecnologia-videojuegos-1/2026/01/19/2552631/coste-plataformas-streaming-dispara-desde-2015-espana.html); [Bango](https://bango.com/europeans-now-spend-almost-e700-on-subscriptions-every-year/)
- Returning a direct debit: 8 weeks for an authorised charge (SEPA Core) and 13 months for an unauthorised one (RDL 19/2018). The bank must refund within 10 business days or give reasons and the complaint routes — [BdE adeudos domiciliados](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/menu-horizontal/productosservici/serviciospago/Adeudos_domiciliados.html); [wwwhatsnew, Oct 2026](https://wwwhatsnew.com/2026/10/27/devolver-recibo-domiciliado-plazos-8-semanas-13-meses/)

### Inferences
- Ranking by euros: public aid (IMV, CAPI, bono social) at billions in aggregate, then IRPF regional deductions (official claimed amount €944M at 30% use suggests a large unused remainder, though the exact gap isn't sourced), then insurance and bank fees (hundreds of euros per case, in the tens of millions), then dormant accounts (about €20-60M a year).
- Ranking by time and friction: inheritances and bank switching (many institutions, deadlines), then cancellations (phone or in person only, retention scripts), then complaints (a 1-month clock, evidence).
- Few people escalate (31k of 1.06M SAC complaints go to the BdE), but claimants do well at the BdE and the DGSFP (about 45% favourable at the DGSFP). Abandonment between steps is the market.

### Gaps
- No official 2024-2025 estimate in euros of IMV or CAPI money not claimed. No official bono social térmico non-take-up figure.
- No official estimate of IRPF regional deductions left unclaimed (Funcas gives a share, not euros; the €9bn figure is commercial).
- No Spanish data on "forgotten subscriptions" (only US studies). No market size for overpriced linked insurance.
- No 2025 DGSFP or CNMV complaint figures were retrieved (the CNMV Memoria 2025 exists: [CNMV](https://www.cnmv.es/DocPortal/Publicaciones/Informes/Memoria_Reclamaciones_2025.pdf), not read).

## 2. Who solves each problem today, what's missing, and the legal limits

### Takeaway
Spain has success-fee legaltech (Reclamador), subscription legal plans (Legálitas), and bank or fintech apps that detect recurring charges (Fintonic, Revolut, N26). Nobody in Spain *executes* the procedure across institutions' websites and phone lines with evidence the other side can't dispute. Pine AI (Singapore, US-focused, $25M raised) is the closest analogue. DoNotPay's FTC case shows the risk of calling yourself a "robot lawyer". Consumers don't need a lawyer for an SAC, BdE or DGSFP complaint, a cancellation or an aid application. That room is open to a tool that acts *as the user's instrument*, with the user signing.

### Cited Findings
- Reclamador works on success fees ("we only charge, if we win, a percentage of what we recover") and claims 90-98% success (self-reported, not specific to any area or year). It acquired "Abogados para tus Deudas" — [Emprendedores](https://emprendedores.es/ideas-de-negocio/reclamaciones-legaltech-companias-aereas); [El Referente](https://elreferente.es/?p=176922); [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/reclamador_es). Reclamador reported revolving-card claims up 125% in H1 2025 — [Bolsamanía](https://www.bolsamania.com/noticias/mercados/economia--las-reclamaciones-a-bancos-por-tarjetas-revolving-aumentan-un-125-hasta-junio-segun-reclamadores--20449278.html)
- Pine AI (19Pine Pte. Ltd., Singapore), founded mid-2024, raised $25M. It calls, negotiates bills, cancels subscriptions and handles insurance appeals; it charges per completed task or on annual credit plans, claims 93% negotiation success (self-reported), has a Spanish-language site, and launched in the US — [SiliconANGLE, May 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/pine-ai-aims-consumer-ai-agent-complex-customer-service-interactions/); [19pine.ai/es](https://www.19pine.ai/es); [TechNexus](https://tecknexus.com/pine-ai-empowering-consumers-with-autonomous-ai-for-customer-service-challenges/2)
- DoNotPay: the FTC finalised an order on 16 Jan 2025 (5-0 vote). It pays $193,000 and is barred from claiming it performs like a lawyer without evidence — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/87474); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/donotpay-has-to-pay-up-over-worlds-first-robot-lawyer-2000503265)
- Fintonic, Revolut and N26 classify recurring charges and flag duplicate or unused subscriptions. Rocket Money (US) is the most cited cancel-from-the-app tool — [wwwhatsnew, Apr 2026](https://wwwhatsnew.com/2026/04/16/suscripciones-digitales-descubrir-gasto-cancelar-innecesarias)
- Cancellations in practice (OCU public complaints, 2026): Holiday Gym only accepted in-person cancellations, from the 1st to the 5th of the month before renewal; Synergym has no phone and no human; VivaGym said there was "no record" of a cancellation request before 12 June 2026 — [OCU Holiday Gym](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/imposibilidad-de-darme-de-baja/c14cc83d894cf5a08d); [OCU trabas baja](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/trabas-para-darse-de-baja-/94e6fa9013858124ed); [OCU VivaGym](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/reclamaci-C3-B3n-contra-vivagym-por/111a59bd6b96bd133f)
- Ley 10/2025 de servicios de atención a la clientela (BOE 27 Dec 2025; companies must adapt by about 27 Dec 2026): complaints accepted through the same channel used to sign up, plus postal, phone and electronic channels; answers through the consumer's channel; notice at least 15 days before automatic renewal, and cancellation made easy; 1-month deadline for complaints; 95% of calls answered within 3 minutes; human attention on request — [Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2026/04/Ley-servicio-de-atencion-a-la-clientela.pdf); [PwC](https://periscopiofiscalylegal.pwc.es/ley-10-2025-de-26-de-diciembre-por-la-que-se-regulan-los-servicios-de-atencion-a-la-clientela/); [Consumo Responde](https://www.consumoresponde.es/art%C3%ADculos/nueva_ley_de_servicios_de_atencion_la_clientela_lsac); [Lawwwing](https://lawwwing.com/ley-de-atencion-a-la-clientela-2025-que-cambia-para-empresas-y-consumidores/)
- EU withdrawal-button Directive 2023/2673: applicable from 19 Jun 2026. The OCU says Spain hasn't transposed it, and the Commission opened infringement proceedings against Spain and 20 other states. El Español claims it is already in the BOE (contradicts the others) — [OCU, 19 Jun 2026](https://www.ocu.org/organizacion/prensa/notas-de-prensa/2026/normabotondesistimiento190626); [elderecho.com](https://elderecho.com/bruselas-expedienta-a-espana-por-no-transponer-la-directiva-sobre-servicios-financieros-a-distancia); [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/omicrono/20260603/va-entrar-vigor-partir-junio-tiendas-online-nuevo-boton-cancelar-compra-facilmente/1003744271473_0.html)
- LO 1/2025 (MASC), in force since 3 Apr 2025: an attempt at out-of-court resolution is required before a civil lawsuit. For consumer disputes, a prior out-of-court complaint, such as one to the bank's SAC, counts. Proof must be "documental fehaciente" (burofax, formal demand). The Audiencia Provincial de Barcelona held that a mere demand announcing legal action does not meet the requirement, so criteria vary by court. The complaint loses its value if no lawsuit follows within 1 year — [PwC MASC y clientes financieros](https://periscopiofiscalylegal.pwc.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Los-MASC-la-nueva-Autoridad-y-los-clientes-financieros.pdf); [AP Barcelona criterios](https://www.conesalegal.com/hubfs/02-Acuerdos-unificacion-criterios-OCTUBRE25_Audiencia-Provincial-de-Barcelona.pdf); [ICAM](https://web.icam.es/ley-organica-1-2025-de-2-de-enero-de-medidas-en-materia-de-eficiencia-del-servicio-publico-de-justicia/); [Economist & Jurist](https://www.economistjurist.es/actualidad-juridica/jurisprudencia/revocada-una-sentencia-que-inadmitio-una-demanda-al-considerar-que-no-se-habia-cumplido-adecuadamente-con-el-requisito-de-procedibilidad/)
- Autoridad de Defensa del Cliente Financiero (a free body to resolve bank complaints within 90 days, funded by a levy on banks): still stuck in Congress. The Government said it would restart the bill in September 2026 — [CCU, 2026](https://consumo-ccu.consumo.gob.es/sites/default/files/notas-de-prensa/DIAMUNDIALDERECHOSPERSONASCONSUMIDORAS2026.pdf); [La Moncloa, Jul 2026](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/serviciosdeprensa/notasprensa/economia-comercio-empresa/Paginas/2026/240726-autoridad-deensa-cliente-financiero.aspx); [Civil-Mercantil](https://www.civil-mercantil.com/node/13888)
- Inheritances (previous note): Heritae (flat fee, human-driven), Testamenta, Everly, Reclamador; bank-by-bank in-house services (Bankinter €85, BBVA, CaixaBank) — [El Referente/Heritae](https://elreferente.es/inversiones/nace-heritae-startup-espanola-gestiona-manera-integral-herencias/); [Bankinter](https://www.bankinter.com/banca/ayuda/tramite-testamento-heredero-cuentas)

### Inferences
- What's missing everywhere: (1) *execution* across the institution's own channels (web form, phone line), not just detection; (2) *evidence* the institution can't dispute: the "no consta su solicitud" problem and the MASC "fehaciente" requirement; (3) *privacy*: Pine or Rocket Money see all your data in clear; (4) Spanish-language phone operation.
- Legal design to avoid intrusismo and DoNotPay-style risk. The product is the consumer's tool (as with filling in your own form), not legal advice or representation in court. The user signs and sends every complaint (human hold). Templates cite the rule but don't say "you will win". For court cases, hand over to a partner lawyer (Reclamador-style success fee). This is an inference, not a legal opinion: whether a company acting as a *representative* before the SAC or BdE needs any licence was not verified.
- For aid applications and tax, third-party filing relies on the user's Cl@ve or certificate, or on registered "colaborador social" and representation schemes. The agent should pre-fill and hold, and the user authenticates.

### Gaps
- No Spanish startup was found that cancels or negotiates by phone on the consumer's behalf. Klar is Mexican; "Sniffer" could not be identified as a Spanish consumer product.
- Reclamador's real volumes, fees and revenue were not found (only self-reported success rates).
- No ruling or regulator text on AI agents acting for consumers before an SAC or the BdE was found.
- The article numbers in RDL 19/2018 for the 8-week and 13-month periods were not verified in the BOE (sources conflict).

## 3. Phone-based processes, and the legality of outbound AI calls (consent, AI Act art. 50, recording)

### Takeaway
Many cancellations and retention flows are still phone-only or in-person only (gyms, telecoms and insurers often push to calls; inheritance and insurance claim lines). Ley 10/2025 requires companies to answer 95% of calls within 3 minutes and to provide a human on request from about December 2026, which makes calling *more* productive for an agent. AI Act art. 50 requires the bot to say it is an AI. Spain allows one-party recording of a call you take part in (STC 114/1984), but distributing the recording is restricted. So a signed recording or transcript is usable as the user's evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Recording: Spain is a one-party-consent jurisdiction for conversations you take part in. The Constitutional Court (STC 114/1984) held that art. 18.3 protects communications against third parties, not against the other participant. Recording a conversation you are not part of is a crime. Publishing a lawful recording can create liability; it may be used personally or as evidence in court — [Reclamador blog](https://www.reclamador.es/blog/es-legal-grabar-una-conversacion-telefonica); [RecordingLaw](https://www.recordinglaw.com/es/world-laws/world-recording-laws/spain-recording-laws/); [TC resolution](https://hj.tribunalconstitucional.es/en/Resolucion/GetDocumentResolucion/6197); [Law&Trends](https://www.lawandtrends.com/noticias/penal/es-legal-grabar-una-conversacion-cuando-si-y-cuando-es-delito-1.html)
- AI Act art. 50, applicable from 2 Aug 2026: the system must disclose that it is an AI at the first interaction; vendor guidance adds saying on whose behalf it calls — [Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-03-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-take-effect-2-august-2026); [Famulor](https://www.famulor.io/nl/blog/eu-ai-act-article-50-what-your-ai-phone-agent-must-say)
- ElevenLabs: native Twilio outbound call endpoint, SIP trunking, webhook tools, Zero Retention Mode (no stored recordings or PII transcripts; API only) — [ElevenLabs outbound API](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/api-reference/twilio/outbound-call.md); [ZRM docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/customization/privacy/zrm)
- Ley 10/2025: 95% of calls answered within 3 minutes; human attention guaranteed on request; purely automated service banned (for companies) — [Consumo Responde](https://www.consumoresponde.es/art%C3%ADculos/nueva_ley_de_servicios_de_atencion_la_clientela_lsac); [Menorca.info](https://www.menorca.info/actualidad/dudas-respuestas/2026/06/05/2642915/adios-largas-esperas-telefonicas-ley-confirma-podran-pasar-mas-minutos-antes-atiendan.html)
- In-person or phone-only cancellation is common in gym complaints (Holiday Gym in-person only; Synergym no phone) — [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/imposibilidad-de-darme-de-baja/c14cc83d894cf5a08d); [OCU](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/trabas-para-darse-de-baja-/94e6fa9013858124ed)
- Pine says it has anti-abuse mechanisms to prevent harassment or excessive automated calling — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/pine-ai-aims-consumer-ai-agent-complex-customer-service-interactions/)

### Inferences
- Legal call design: the opening line discloses AI status and principal ("Soy un asistente de IA que llama en nombre de [titular], que lo ha autorizado"); the consent record is stored and signed; the recording is made by the *participant* side (the user's agent), so it is one-party lawful; the transcript is hashed and signed, for evidence only and never published; warm transfer to the user when identity checks need the account holder. Under ZRM, the audio never stays with ElevenLabs; the signed evidence lives with the user (Priv8 expediente).
- These are consumer-initiated service calls, not commercial calls, so telemarketing rules (spam, Robinson list) should not apply. This is an inference; the LGTel was not checked.
- Recorded calls plus Ley 10/2025's obligations turn "they never let me cancel" into evidence: wait time (> 3 min), refusal of a human, refusal to accept the cancellation through the sign-up channel.

### Gaps
- No list was found of which Spanish banks or insurers require a phone call for specific procedures (for example cancelling a linked insurance policy, or a card dispute). This is anecdotal.
- Whether the institution's representative can refuse to deal with an AI agent, and whether Ley 10/2025 says anything about consumer-side bots, was not verified.
- GDPR status of recording the company's agent (a data subject) when used only as personal evidence was not checked in AEPD guidance.

## 4. Concepts (mandatory format), reusing Priv8 and 8, B2B2C

### Takeaway
The five strongest concepts all combine an agent that *executes* through institutions' websites and phone lines with Priv8's evidence and privacy primitives. In every case the moat is the signed proof, not the chatbot: tokenised data, provenance policy, human hold and a signed expediente. Best overall: **"Prueba de Baja" (cancellations and linked-insurance exits by phone with signed evidence)**, about 4.25/5, then **"Albacea que llama"** at about 4.1 and **"Mudanza 8"** at about 4.0. None is an "AI finance assistant" or an "elderly anti-scam assistant": each one *does* a legally defined procedure and leaves verifiable evidence.

### Inferences

#### Concept A. Prueba de Baja: "The agent that cancels by phone and gives you proof they can't deny"
1. **One line:** You say "cancel my gym, my bank's home insurance and Movistar Plus". 8 calls or fills in each company's form, says it is an AI acting for you, records the call legally as a participant, and gives you a signed expediente. If they later say "no consta", the expediente is your SAC complaint or MASC proof.
2. **Problem:** Companies demand in-person or phone-only cancellation, or deny there was a request ([OCU Holiday Gym](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/imposibilidad-de-darme-de-baja/c14cc83d894cf5a08d), [OCU VivaGym](https://www.ocu.org/reclamar/lista-reclamaciones-publicas/reclamaci-C3-B3n-contra-vivagym-por/111a59bd6b96bd133f)). Streaming prices are up 81.7% since 2015 ([Menorca.info](https://www.menorca.info/actualidad/tecnologia-videojuegos-1/2026/01/19/2552631/coste-plataformas-streaming-dispara-desde-2015-espana.html)). Bank-linked insurance is often overpriced and the market is "captive" ([OCU](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/hipotecas/informe/hipotecas-y-seguros)). Ley 10/2025 makes companies accept cancellation through the sign-up channel and answer within 3 min from about 27 Dec 2026 ([Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2026/04/Ley-servicio-de-atencion-a-la-clientela.pdf)). Spain still hasn't transposed the EU withdrawal button ([OCU](https://www.ocu.org/organizacion/prensa/notas-de-prensa/2026/normabotondesistimiento190626)).
3. **Who pays:** B2B2C. (a) Consumer associations (OCU, FACUA) as a member benefit, priced per member per year or per completed cancellation. (b) Banks and neobanks as a retention feature: "cancel recurring charges from your account", which pairs with Concept C. (c) Comparators (Selectra, Rastreator, HelpMyCash) that earn a commission when the user switches insurer or telco: cancelling the old contract is their conversion bottleneck. Market size: Europeans spend about €700 a year on subscriptions ([Bango](https://bango.com/europeans-now-spend-almost-e700-on-subscriptions-every-year/)); Pine raised $25M on the same thesis in the US ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/06/pine-ai-aims-consumer-ai-agent-complex-customer-service-interactions/)). No Spanish market size was found.
4. **Competition:** Pine AI (US, English-first, no signed evidence, sees data in clear); Rocket Money and Trim (US, bank aggregation plus a concierge); Fintonic, Revolut and N26 (detect, don't execute) ([wwwhatsnew](https://wwwhatsnew.com/2026/04/16/suscripciones-digitales-descubrir-gasto-cancelar-innecesarias)); burofax services (evidence, but no execution and slow). What they lack: Spanish phone operation, legally framed evidence (one-party recording plus signed hash plus Ley 10/2025 checks) and privacy (tokens).
5. **What we build and reuse:** Priv8 tokens (DNI, IBAN and policy number spoken by the local executor only into the audio leg); provenance (the retention agent's offers can't change the instruction: "cancel" stays "cancel", and a counter-offer only goes to the user as a question); human hold (accepting a retention offer or paying a penalty); signed expediente (consent, call timestamps, wait time, transcript hash, the company's words "su baja queda registrada con número X"). 8's voice and eyes for the user.
6. **Novelty:** Cancellation as *evidence production*. No product produces a call record built to serve as the user's evidence that Ley 10/2025 was breached (wait over 3 min, human refused, channel refused) and that feeds straight into an SAC complaint or MASC.
7. **Frontier AI and audio:** Claude plans per company (finds the channel, the notice period, the 15-day renewal notice rule), runs the web form through computer use, and writes the complaint if the cancellation fails. ElevenLabs makes the outbound call over Twilio under ZRM, with Scribe STT to catch the confirmation number. **Metrics:** user minutes saved (hold plus call), cancellation confirmed rate over N test calls, % of calls with a confirmation number extracted, latency per turn, and the share of PII tokens that never reached the LLM (target 100%).
8. **3-minute demo and 1-3 day build:** On stage, a live call to a "VivaGym" line played by a second ElevenLabs agent with a retention script. 8 discloses that it is an AI, resists two counter-offers, gets a confirmation number, and the screen shows the transcript with tokens next to the audio with real data, then the signed expediente PDF. Then a fake "no consta" email arrives, and 8 builds the SAC complaint with the evidence attached. Real: Twilio, ElevenLabs, Claude, signing. Simulated: the company side.
9. **Sponsor fit:** ElevenLabs (high: the phone call is essential), Claude (high), MyInvestor (medium, as a feature when switching: cancel old-bank products), GPTadvisor (low to medium: audit trails).
10. **Scores:** Innovation and viability 4 (Pine proves demand; the evidence angle and Spain are new); Frontier AI and audio 5 (voice is indispensable); UX and accessibility 4; Implementation 4 (narrow and demoable); Presentation 5 (a dramatic live call). **Weighted 4.25.**
11. **Risks:** The company may refuse to deal with an AI and insist on the account holder (mitigation: warm transfer). Legal reading of the recording as evidence: one-party recording is lawful, but its weight depends on the court. Ley 10/2025 obligations only apply from about Dec 2026, and financial services are partly carved out ([Management Solutions](https://www.managementsolutions.com/sites/default/files/publicaciones/esp/nt-boe-lsac.pdf)). Abuse (harassment calls) needs rate limits.
12. **Sources:** inline above.

#### Concept B. Albacea que llama: "The heir's agent that finds every account and policy, makes the calls, and beats the 6-month clock"
1. **One line:** It extends the earlier "Albacea" idea with a phone layer and a dormant-asset sweep. 8 requests the certificates, calls or writes to each candidate bank and insurer to ask for balance-at-death certificates, flags fees charged unduly (testamentaría fees), and tracks the 6-month modelo 650 clock.
2. **Problem:** About 441,000 deaths a year ([INE](https://ine.es/dyngs/Prensa/pEDCM2025.htm)); 6 months for Sucesiones ([AEAT](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/declaraciones-informativas-otros-impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-sucesiones-donaciones/adquisiciones-mortis-causa-modelo-650/documentacion-aportar.html)); inheritances are the 5th BdE complaint topic; "only Hacienda knows" which accounts existed ([OCU](https://www.ocu.org/dinero/herencia/consejos/cuenta-fallecido)); dormant accounts pass to the State after 20 years, €199.7M in 2018-2023 ([The Objective](https://theobjective.com/economia/2025-10-01/hacienda-cuentas-abandonadas-ingreso-200-millones-2018/)).
3. **Who pays:** B2B2C via (a) funeral (decesos) insurers, whose policies often bundle "gestión testamentaria" (UNVERIFIED: the decesos market size was not sourced in this round); (b) banks' testamentaría desks (Bankinter charges €85 ([Bankinter](https://www.bankinter.com/banca/ayuda/tramite-testamento-heredero-cuentas))); (c) gestorías and notaries as a pre-filled case file. Heritae's €999-2,999 price point shows consumer willingness to pay ([El Referente](https://elreferente.es/inversiones/nace-heritae-startup-espanola-gestiona-manera-integral-herencias/)) (UNCERTAIN on the figures).
4. **Competition:** Heritae, Testamenta, Everly, Reclamador (human-driven); banks' own forms (single entity). What they lack: multi-institution orchestration, calls, and provenance on every euro that goes into the 650.
5. **What we build and reuse:** all of Priv8. Tokens for the deceased's and heirs' identities; an oracle ("does the balance on the certificate match what the bank said on the phone?"); provenance (a PDF from a bank can't inject instructions); human hold (signing at the notary, filing the 650); signed expediente for the gestor. 8's voice for grieving or elderly heirs.
6. **Novelty:** No one orchestrates across Justice, the banks, insurers and the AEAT/CCAA with calls and evidence. Flagging the BdE's position that fees are only justified for genuine advisory turns into a complaint automatically.
7. **Frontier AI and audio:** Claude reads wills, certificates and statements, builds the inventory and drafts requests; ElevenLabs makes the calls to bank inheritance desks and explains each step to the heir. **Metrics:** days to a full asset map vs a baseline; institutions contacted; € located; fees contested; voice task completion for users over 65.
8. **Demo and build:** A mocked Ministry of Justice certificate plus 3 mock banks (one with only a phone line), a live call to the bank mock, the inventory dashboard with a countdown, a pre-filled 650 draft held for signature. 2-3 days; government portals are simulated.
9. **Sponsor fit:** MyInvestor (released money gets reinvested; an account for heirs), GPTadvisor (advisor-side case file), ElevenLabs, Claude.
10. **Scores:** Innovation 4 (Heritae exists, but not agentic); AI and audio 5; UX 5; Implementation 3 (many mocked portals); Presentation 5 (emotional story). **Weighted about 4.1.**
11. **Risks:** Banks require the heir's identity and documents in person; legal representation limits; the demo is heavy on simulation; decesos B2B channel unverified.
12. **Sources:** inline above and in `inversion_herencias_reclamaciones.md`.

#### Concept C. Mudanza 8: "Switch bank in one conversation: account, direct debits, linked insurance, funds and plans"
1. **One line:** When a user opens MyInvestor, 8 runs the whole move. It starts the legal account switch (13 business days), lists every direct debit, flags linked insurance and compares discount against premium *without revealing the figures* (oracle), calls the old bank's insurer to cancel or claim the unused premium, and pre-fills fund and pension transfers.
2. **Problem:** The switching rails exist (RDL 19/2017: at most 13 business days, free ([BOE](https://boe.es/buscar/pdf/2017/BOE-A-2017-13644-consolidado.pdf)); funds in 5-8 business days ([CNMV](https://www.cnmv.es/DocPortal/Publicaciones/Fichas/TraspasosEntreFondosDeInversion.pdf))), but linked insurance stays behind: bank policies are often more expensive ([HelpMyCash](https://www.helpmycash.com/blog/los-seguros-vinculados-a-la-hipoteca-no-son-obligatorios/)), the unused premium refund on early repayment is often unclaimed ([Selectra](https://selectra.es/seguros/noticias/hipoteca-seguro-2026-banco-no-puede-imponer)), and the Supreme Court found financed single-premium clauses abusive ([Finantres](https://finantresnoticias.com/bancos/supremo-primas-unicas-hipotecas-catalanes/)).
3. **Who pays:** The receiving bank (MyInvestor and other neobanks) as an acquisition tool, paid per completed switch. Inflow KPI: about €400M a month at MyInvestor ([Finect](https://www.finect.com/usuario/mariarefojos/articulos/myinvestor-supera-el-millon-de-clientes-en-2026-ya-capta-400-millones-al-mes-via-inversion)).
4. **Competition:** BBVA's "app traspasos" for pension plans ([BBVA](https://www.bbva.es/personas/productos/planes-de-pensiones/app-traspasos.html)); banks' own switching forms. Nobody handles the insurance and recurring-charge tail or the old bank's phone-only steps.
5. **What we build and reuse:** tokens (IBANs, policy numbers); oracle (is the premium saving greater than the spread penalty? It answers yes or no without exposing the numbers); provenance (old-bank retention offers can't redirect the transfer); human hold on every signature; signed expediente. Open2Agents mandates if transfers are executed.
6. **Novelty:** The first switch that includes *undoing the old bank's tie-ins* by phone and web, with evidence, rather than just moving direct debits.
7. **Frontier AI and audio:** Claude reads the old bank's statements and policies (PDF and DOM) and builds the "moving map"; ElevenLabs calls the old insurer. **Metrics:** recurring items migrated vs missed; € per year of premium saved; elapsed days vs the 13-day legal maximum; user taps vs the manual flow.
8. **Demo and build:** A mock old-bank portal plus a mock insurer phone line; the MyInvestor MCP catalogue (public) to show the destination funds the *user* picks ("ejecuta, no aconseja"); the transfer order held in a "finish in app" screen. 2 days.
9. **Sponsor fit:** MyInvestor (very high), GPTadvisor (high), ElevenLabs (medium to high), Claude (high).
10. **Scores:** Innovation 4; AI and audio 4; UX 4; Implementation 4; Presentation 4. **Weighted 4.0.**
11. **Risks:** Overlaps with the team's current "Mandato 8" (transfers), so it should be framed as its extension. Spread penalties vary by contract (UNCERTAIN 0.10-0.60 points ([iAhorro](https://www.iahorro.com/blog/educacion-financiera/cancelar-seguro-vinculado-hipoteca))). MyInvestor has no mortgage-switch angle verified.
12. **Sources:** inline.

#### Concept D. Reclamo con Prueba: "From an undue charge to a court-ready claim, built for you and signed by you"
1. **One line:** 8 watches the user's movements (tokenised), detects a returnable direct debit (8 weeks or 13 months), an undue fee or a refused card dispute, files the return or the SAC complaint, tracks the 1-month clock, escalates to the BdE or the DGSFP, and produces a dossier that is "documental fehaciente" for the MASC requirement before suing.
2. **Problem:** 1.06M SAC complaints, 58% rejected, and only about 7% escalated to the BdE, although claimants recover an average of €625 there ([Roams](https://roams.es/actualidad/finanzas/reclamaciones-banca-caen-2025-fraude-lidera-quejas/); [BdE](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/blog/servicio-de-reclamaciones-del-banco-de-espana-que-es-y-que-datos-deja-la-memoria-de-2025.html)). The DGSFP finds for the claimant in 44.7% of cases ([Finreg360](https://finreg360.com/alerta/la-direccion-general-de-seguros-y-fondos-de-pensiones-publica-la-memoria-de-reclamaciones-de-2024/)). MASC has been required since April 2025, with diverging court criteria on what proof counts ([PwC](https://periscopiofiscalylegal.pwc.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Los-MASC-la-nueva-Autoridad-y-los-clientes-financieros.pdf); [AP Barcelona](https://www.conesalegal.com/hubfs/02-Acuerdos-unificacion-criterios-OCTUBRE25_Audiencia-Provincial-de-Barcelona.pdf)). Direct-debit return windows are 8 weeks or 13 months ([BdE](https://clientebancario.bde.es/pcb/es/menu-horizontal/productosservici/serviciospago/Adeudos_domiciliados.html)).
3. **Who pays:** (a) Law firms and legaltech (Reclamador-type) buy pre-qualified, evidence-complete cases (cost per acquisition plus a success share); (b) consumer associations as a member benefit; (c) consumers pay a success fee only on amounts below what lawyers will take on. The future Autoridad de Defensa del Cliente Financiero, if it passes, would add a free 90-day route ([CCU](https://consumo-ccu.consumo.gob.es/sites/default/files/notas-de-prensa/DIAMUNDIALDERECHOSPERSONASCONSUMIDORAS2026.pdf)).
4. **Competition:** Reclamador (success-fee lawyers, high-value cases), OCU's complaint platform (public complaints, no execution), DoNotPay (US, sanctioned by the FTC ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/node/87474))). What's missing: small-ticket claims (€20-600) that no lawyer takes, done end to end with provenance.
5. **What we build and reuse:** tokens over bank movements; provenance (each fact in the claim is linked to the source movement or document); human hold (the user signs and sends); signed expediente as MASC proof. Computer use on the BdE complaints portal.
6. **Novelty:** Each claim carries *verifiable provenance*: every number traces to a signed source. It is built to meet the MASC proof standard and the regulator's format, for claims too small for lawyers.
7. **Frontier AI and audio:** Claude classifies the charge, matches it to the rule (RDL 19/2018, BdE criteria) and drafts; voice for intake ("explain what happened") and for calling the SAC phone line when needed. **Metrics:** € claimable found per user; time to file (minutes vs hours); % of claims with complete evidence; deadlines met.
8. **Demo and build:** A mock bank statement with a suspicious €39 fee and an unauthorised direct debit; 8 finds them, files the SEPA return (mock), drafts the SAC complaint with a provenance tree, the clock runs forward 30 days, and it fills in the BdE form. 1-2 days.
9. **Sponsor fit:** Claude (high); ElevenLabs (medium: voice is helpful but not essential); MyInvestor (low, since banks are the defendant); GPTadvisor (low).
10. **Scores:** Innovation 4; AI and audio 3; UX 4; Implementation 4; Presentation 4. **Weighted about 3.85.**
11. **Risks:** Weak sponsor fit; intrusismo if it is perceived as legal advice (DoNotPay precedent); MASC criteria vary by court.
12. **Sources:** inline.

#### Concept E. Lo que te toca: "8 finds the aid and deductions you're owed and files them, without ever seeing your income"
1. **One line:** With tokenised income and household data, Priv8's oracle checks eligibility thresholds without revealing the numbers (IMV, CAPI, bono social eléctrico and térmico, regional IRPF deductions, including retroactive rectifications for the last 4 years). 8 pre-fills the Seguridad Social or comercializadora de referencia forms and Renta Web boxes, holds for the user's Cl@ve signature, and calls the supplier to activate the bono social.
2. **Problem:** IMV non-take-up 56% ([The Objective/AIReF](https://theobjective.com/economia/2024-07-10/airef-beneficiarios-ingreso-minimo-2023/)); CAPI about 72%, with only 23% reached in 2024 ([El Salto](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/renta-basica/55percent-hogares-podrian-recibir-imv-no-lo-han-solicitado-segun-airef)); bono social: 1.73M beneficiaries against up to 3.5M more eligible, with 42.5-57.5% discounts until Dec 2026 ([Selectra](https://selectra.es/energia/info/bono-social/beneficiarios); [Vozpópuli](https://www.vozpopuli.com/economia/energia/fiasco-bono-social-luz-35-millones-hogares-no-reciben-complejidad.html)); regional deductions under 30% used ([Consumer/Funcas](https://www.consumer.es/economia-domestica/los-contribuyentes-apenas-se-deducen-el-30-del-importe-maximo-posible-de-las-deducciones-del-irpf.html)), not pre-filled ([Merca2](https://www.merca2.es/2026/06/07/irpf-deduccion-autonomica-casilla-2389453/)), and recoverable retroactively ([El Mirón de Soria](https://elmirondesoria.es/cyl/junta-de-castilla-y-leon/la-junta-subraya-ventajas-fiscales-de-la-comunidad/amp)).
3. **Who pays:** B2B2C via (a) banks' social and financial-inclusion programmes, such as payroll accounts for low-income clients (the Government's Foro de Inclusión Financiera ([La Moncloa](https://www.lamoncloa.gob.es/serviciosdeprensa/notasprensa/economia-comercio-empresa/Paginas/2026/240726-autoridad-deensa-cliente-financiero.aspx))); (b) municipalities' social services and NGOs (per case handled); (c) utilities that must manage the bono social; (d) gestorías, for the deductions part (per return). No market size was found.
4. **Competition:** TaxDown and gestorías (tax filing, but deductions depend on what the user declares); comparators (Selectra) with guides; AIReF itself proposes granting the IMV automatically, which is a threat if adopted. What's missing: one place that checks *every* entitlement privately and files it.
5. **What we build and reuse:** tokens and the **oracle** (the star feature here: "income below threshold? yes or no" without the LLM seeing the income); provenance (official rule text from the BOE or regional bulletin as the only allowed source for thresholds); human hold for Cl@ve; signed expediente of what was filed.
6. **Novelty:** Private eligibility checking (zero-knowledge in spirit) plus execution of the claim, including phone calls to suppliers. Retroactive deduction recovery is rarely offered.
7. **Frontier AI and audio:** Claude reads the rules (more than 300 regional deductions) and maps them to the household; voice intake in plain Spanish for people with low digital skills. **Metrics:** € a year identified per household; forms pre-filled; % of PII withheld from the LLM; time to complete vs a baseline.
8. **Demo and build:** 3 personas (a family in Extremadura, a pensioner, a gig worker); oracle checks; € found on screen; Renta Web mock with boxes ticked; a bono social call to a mock comercializadora. 2-3 days (the rule base for 2-3 regions only).
9. **Sponsor fit:** Claude (high), ElevenLabs (medium to high), MyInvestor (low), GPTadvisor (low to medium).
10. **Scores:** Innovation 4; AI and audio 4; UX 5; Implementation 3 (the rule base is laborious); Presentation 5 (social impact). **Weighted about 3.95.**
11. **Risks:** Close to "AI finance assistant" if pitched poorly, so frame it as "files your claims", not "advises you"; rule accuracy; Cl@ve can't be automated; aid rules changing in 2026.
12. **Sources:** inline.
